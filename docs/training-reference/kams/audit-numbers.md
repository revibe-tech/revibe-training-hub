The "database score AE" sheet on frame t00535 gives scored price = price x (1 - score): for SF, 24 x (1 - 0.04) = 23.04. The glossary row ("price times (1 minus score)") matches the database sheet, and the topic 3 row matches the parameters sheet. The two sheets may use the score with opposite signs, and the page does not say which applies to a buyer. Not changed; needs a check against the live sheet.

The current `topics/11-dashboard.md` contains no such sentence (grep for "31 Aug" and "30 Aug" finds nothing in topic 11). The earlier fix cannot be reproduced against the current file, so nothing is changed here.
