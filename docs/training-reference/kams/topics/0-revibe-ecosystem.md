## How it works

1. The seller ecosystem has two parts: Onboarding (left) and Journey (right) (0.0 [00:18]).
2. Onboarding App: the seller completes legal clearance, accepts Revibe terms and conditions, and reads the quality guidelines (0.0 [00:29]) (0.0 [00:20 t00020]).
3. Legal and admin clearance: marked Auto, mostly automatic, though the KAM may need to guide the seller. The seller provides five documents (0.0 [02:36]) (0.0 [02:43]) (0.0 [02:59]).
4. Quality check: the seller sends 20 to 30 pieces to the Revibe lab. Revibe inspects them and gives feedback. The purpose is to align the seller's quality with Revibe's standard (0.0 [03:09]) (0.0 [03:15]) (0.0 [00:49]). Sellers move to the quality check after legal and admin clearance (0.0 [03:03]).
5. Outcome: onboarded if the quality is good, rejected if it is not. If most of the products are rejected, the seller cannot proceed with the application (0.0 [01:04]) (0.0 [00:58]).
6. Interview (1h): the KAM learns the seller's business (supply origin, retail experience, warranty policy, technical team, online experience) and presents Revibe's main assets (0.0 [01:30]) (0.0 [04:01]).
7. Account creation and sales (1h): creation of all Revibe tool access and launch of sales. Trainings then take one day, covering Buybox, Dashboard, Orders app, Claims app and Fulfillment app (0.0 [03:24]) (0.0 [03:27]).
8. Trial period (2 weeks): close monitoring, regular Q&A and training sessions. The KAM trains the seller and checks that the seller follows the operation guidelines (0.0 [03:44]) (0.0 [03:39]) (0.0 [03:54]).
9. Journey: the seller receives orders through Buybox (sets prices for direct shipping) or the Fulfillment App (sets prices and quantities for fulfillment shipping). Orders are handled in the Revibe App, and the Seller portal gives access to all apps and trainings (0.0 [05:15]) (0.0 [05:39]) (0.0 [06:05]) (0.0 [06:20]).
10. A scoring system on the seller dashboard affects the seller's prices. Its arrows feed back to Buybox and Fulfillment App (0.0 [06:34]).
11. The KAM's main goal is to build a relationship with the seller and talk with them regularly (0.0 [01:12]) (0.0 [01:15]).
