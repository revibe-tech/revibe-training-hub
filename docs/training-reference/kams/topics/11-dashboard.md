# 11. Dashboard

## What this topic covers

Two training videos on the Revibe performance dashboards. Each citation gives its item id and the video timestamp.

- **11.0 (KAMs, 29:58):** the Supply dashboard for key account managers. It covers the Daily tab (morning checklist), the KAM tab (own book, performance and bonus), the Sellers tab (one seller at a time), the Scores tab (weekly Thursday routine) and the Tools forms. Team and Price recs get a quick look. KPIs and Periskope are empty placeholders for now (11.0 [29:32] [29:35]).
- **11.1 (Sellers, about 9 min 10 s):** the Seller Performance dashboard that sellers sign in to. It covers the header, the Performance tab (timeframes, filters, KPI tiles, Orders, GMV, On time, Defect rate, Days to solve, Claims coverage), the Scoring tab's next score table and the Analysis tab (11.1 [00:19] [00:54] [05:18] [06:19]).

Cautions for anyone teaching this topic:

- Every dashboard screen in 11.1 shows a demo seller (Seller ABC) on a localhost demo build, and its figures are sample data that must not be read as a current score (11.1 [00:48] [05:01]).
- The 11.0 figures are snapshots dated 2026-10-16 (11.0 Summary; 11.0 [08:46]).
- The 11.0 KAM pages show staff pay and KPI data, and Price recs shows competitor cost quotes. Do not show these to sellers (11.0 Personal data on screen).

## How it works

One walkthrough, in the order a user meets the process. Steps 1 to 13 are the KAM version (11.0). Steps 14 to 23 are the seller version (11.1).

**KAM version (11.0)**

1. **Header and tabs.** The header has Meeting notes, Data freshness and Home. The tab bar reads Daily, Periskope, Team, KAM, Sellers, Price recs, KPIs, Scores, Tools (11.0 [00:24] [00:45] [00:56]).
2. **Freshness.** Numbers are refreshed every night, so the morning view reflects the early-morning cut. If a number looks wrong after a fix, check again the next morning (11.0 [01:00] [01:11]).
3. **Daily tab (morning checklist).** Work down the list from top to bottom every morning (11.0 [03:41]). The sections are Meetings, Legal, Buybox closed, Top late orders, Stuck orders, Unidentified carrier, Refund policy and Claims coverage. The number beside each line is how many items are waiting (11.0 [03:28]). Narrow the list with the account manager tabs and the status buttons (11.0 [03:49] [03:56]).
4. **Morning routine.** Check meetings, chase legal documents, reopen closed buyboxes, push late and stuck orders, fix missing carriers and close returns before they breach the policy (11.0 [10:16] [10:23]).
5. **Meetings and legal.** Log every call or visit in Meeting notes straight after it (11.0 [04:52] [04:56]). Legal is a chase list: anything marked missing or crossed is collected from the seller (11.0 [05:29]).
6. **Buybox closed.** Lists active sellers closed for more than three days. The table shows Since, Last meeting, Rationale and four score columns (11.0 [05:40] [06:00] [06:07] [06:13]).
7. **Late orders.** Confirmed orders not yet shipped, still at Order created or Quality check, at least two days old. Call the oldest first (11.0 [06:36] [06:40] [06:57]).
8. **Stuck orders and carriers.** Stuck orders are shipped orders with no delivered date. Mark delivered orders as delivered, because until then they count as late on the on-time score (11.0 [07:10] [07:41]). Unidentified carrier lists shipped orders whose tracking record names no carrier. Ask the seller for the real courier and tracking number (11.0 [07:58] [08:19]).
9. **Refund policy and claims coverage.** The policy clock counts working hours in stages the seller owns. Moving means the seller holds the claim and the clock runs. Stopped means the claim is on Revibe's side and the clock is frozen. Push sellers on returns in the warning columns before the policy is applied (11.0 [08:34] [09:12] [09:32]). Claims coverage bars show closed (green) and still open (red) claims per month. Click a bar for its claims (11.0 [09:44] [10:05]).
10. **KAM tab.** Click your own name in the account manager row, and every section below shows only your sellers. The index runs HR, Sellers, Orders, On time, Fulfillment, Quality, Claims management, Cancellation, Sellers connections, Meetings and Legal (11.0 [10:49] [10:51] [10:55] [10:58]).
11. **HR and bonus.** Each bonus period runs from the 20th of the previous month to the 19th. The four tiles and the KPI table show the current position. The defect rate is read one period back (M-1). Bonus baseline is the monthly variable salary, and Bonus reached is the total multiplied by that baseline. The Manual column and Ship to payroll are greyed out for the KAM (11.0 [11:21] [11:31] [11:49] [12:50] [12:58]).
12. **Sellers, Orders, On time, Quality, Claims management on the KAM tab.** Active sellers have 30 or more orders in the month and semi-active sellers have 1 to 29. Use Slowing down and read the PACE percentage, not the raw count. Orders and GMV show per period. On time shows promises per market. Quality uses the 30-day and 90-day defect rates. Claims management covers days to solve, refund policy and invalid claims (11.0 [13:26] [13:42] [14:20] [14:32] [14:52] [17:48] [18:32] [18:55] [19:03]). Cancellation and Sellers connections are narrated only (11.0 [19:26] [19:33]).
13. **Sellers tab.** Search for one seller. The summary tiles show that seller over the whole window. Before every seller call, open the seller's page here (11.0 [20:29] [20:35] [23:52] [23:55]). The KAM view is the same sections as the KAM tab, filtered to one seller. Clicking the tab a second time shows the seller view, which is not shown in any frame (11.0 [21:00] [21:07]).
14. **Scores tab (weekly routine).** Scores update every Thursday. Pepe checks the scores and makes justified manual updates, emails all account managers with the week's moves, and updates the scores so the new ones go live. A new score below minus 5% gets the Code broken tick automatically. Step 4 (closing buyboxes below minus 5%) is narrated only (11.0 [25:22] [25:51] [25:58] [26:07] [26:16] [26:23]). Step 5 belongs to the KAM: adjust scores in Tools, Seller info, with a rationale, and reopen buyboxes for sellers who have fixed their issues (11.0 [26:32] [26:38]).
15. **Tools tab.** Three forms: Meeting notes (seller, Created By, date, notes, Submit), Seller onboarding and Seller info. Seller info holds status, account manager, contacts, bank details, courier keys and legal documents, and it is where the score adjustment is entered (new score, rationale, Restart, Recover or Correction, save). Keeping seller information current clears the Legal list (11.0 [27:12] [27:38] [28:22] [28:32]). Close a panel with its cross, or use New tab for a full-screen view (11.0 [28:42]).
16. **Team and Price recs (glance only).** Team has the KAM tab's sections for the whole team. Use the KAM tab day to day (11.0 [28:57] [29:08]). Price recs lists the few buybox cost problems worth acting on. Rows appear when live buybox costs jumped against last week or last month on products with real demand. Each row names the seller to call and the target price, ranked by GMV at risk (11.0 [29:11] [29:24]).
17. **Closing routine.** Start with the Daily tab, follow your book in the KAM tab, prepare each call in the Sellers tab, act on Thursday's scores and log every meeting in Tools (11.0 [29:43] [29:49]).

**Seller version (11.1)**

18. **Sign-in.** Sign in with Google (using the Google address Revibe holds for the seller), or choose Sign in with email instead. The email and password are the same as for admin.revibe.me, and there is no separate password for this dashboard (11.1 [00:19] [00:25] [00:27]).
19. **Header.** Shows the seller name, a Buybox open or closed pill, the account manager pill, a WhatsApp button and a Meeting button. Sign out is at the top right (11.1 [00:38] [00:48]).
20. **Tabs.** Performance, Scoring and Analysis (11.1 [00:54]).
21. **Performance filters.** Timeframe is Monthly (last 12M), Weekly (last 12W) or Daily (last 30D). Country is All, Exclude SA or Core 3. Category buttons include iPhone, Samsung, Macbook, Laptop, Tablet, Watch, All and Top 3 (11.1 [01:07] [01:11] [01:14] [01:27]).
22. **Top KPI tiles.** Seven tiles, each compared with the previous period. For a month the comparison is against the previous month, for a week against the previous week and for a day against the previous day (11.1 [01:17] [01:23] [01:51]).
23. **Sections.** Orders counts confirmed order lines by the day each was confirmed. GMV has the same controls. On time has three toggles: On time, On time shipment and On time delivery. Defect rate shows the total rate and the 30-day and 90-day rates. Days to solve splits by All claims, Returns and Warranties. Claims coverage shows solved and still-open claims (11.1 [01:34] [02:00] [02:15] [02:40] [04:58] [05:01]). Each KPI has a Methodology button (11.1 [01:51] [02:13] [02:36] [03:03] [05:03] [05:03]).
24. **Scoring tab.** The next score table lists the output of the next score calculation. Its columns are Metric, Period, KPI, Score impact, Weight and Contribution, with a Next score row. Click the On time metric name for the detail behind it (11.1 [05:18] [05:26] [05:57]).
25. **Analysis tab.** Shows the detail for each KPI. On time lists each missed order with its miss tag and working-day figures. Defect rate lists claims by reported issue and model. Days to solve shows claim-level endings. Claims coverage lists claims still open (11.1 [06:10] [06:21] [07:28] [08:08] [08:26]).
26. **Questions.** Any question about a number, or a figure that does not match the seller portal, goes to the account manager, by WhatsApp or by booking a meeting in the account manager's agenda (11.1 [08:57] [09:04] [09:44]).

## Seller version vs KAM version

The topic has two versions, one for KAMs and one for sellers.

**The KAM version (11.0) adds:**

- The Daily tab as a morning chase routine, covering meetings, legal, buyboxes, late and stuck orders, carriers and refund policy across all sellers.
- The KAM tab for the KAM's own book, with HR performance, bonus and career sections, plus cross-seller views of orders, on time, fulfillment, quality, claims management, cancellation and seller connections.
- The Scores tab's weekly routine, in which the KAM makes step 5 adjustments (11.0 [26:28] [26:44]).
- The Tools forms for meeting notes, seller onboarding and seller info.
- Team and Price recs, both as glances (11.0 [28:52] [29:11]).
- Sellers connections, a monitor of how often each seller opens the seller portal (11.0 [19:45] [20:01]).

**The seller version (11.1) shows:**

- One seller's own figures, with the same KPIs and their definitions.
- No bonus, payroll or internal lists.
- Next score, which is the seller's own score calculation on the Scoring tab (11.1 [05:26]).
- A route for questions: the account manager, through WhatsApp or a booked meeting (11.1 [09:04]).

**Shared concepts with different detail:**

- **Claims coverage.** 11.0 shows closed (green) against still-open (red) claims per month, and says the closed share is the coverage (11.0 [09:44]). It does not state a divisor. 11.1 defines coverage as solved claims over claims to solve, where the divisor is received minus cancelled. It is keyed on the repair partner, and change-of-mind and invalid claims are excluded (11.1 [05:02]).
- **Defect rate.** The KAM Performance card reads the defect rate one period back (M-1) (11.0 [11:49]). 11.1 uses the same claim rule over confirmed orders, with the 30-day and 90-day rates settling only after their windows close (11.1 [03:03]).
- **Weights.** The KAM Performance card uses GMV 50%, new active sellers 0%, on time 10%, defect rate (M-1) 30% and days to solve 10% (11.0 [12:06]). The seller next score uses on time 10%, defect rate 60%, days to solve 20% and claims coverage 10% (11.1 [05:26]). These are two different tables and the pages do not say which one drives the Scores tab. See Contradictions.

## Contradictions

**Within 11.0**

- **Stuck orders threshold.** The Daily section says shipped at least four days ago (11.0 [07:10]). The KAM section says shipped more than three days ago (11.0 [16:11]). The page itself flags this; confirm the live rule before teaching it.
- **Scoring weights.** The KAM Performance card uses defect rate 30% and days to solve 10%, with GMV at 50% and no claims coverage (11.0 [12:06]). The Scores tab says each seller is scored on four KPIs: on time, defect rate, days to solve and claims coverage (11.0 [24:20]). The Scores tab weights are not shown in 11.0.
- **Who makes the manual updates.** The weekly routine names Pepe for steps 1 to 4 (11.0 [25:58] [26:23]). The Manual column and Ship to payroll are for Pierre (11.0 [13:06]). Neither page says whether these are the same person. Do not assign them to one name without confirmation.
- **Mekko definition.** Narration says width is the share of orders and height the share of claims (11.0 [23:11]). The on-screen text says width is the category's share of the claims shown, and each cell's area is its claim count (11.0 [23:04]). The narration is not resolved, so teach the on-screen text.
- **Meetings count.** The Meetings card total reads 100 in some frames and 95 in others (11.0 Problems, unclear passages). The reason is not narrated.
- **Late orders statuses.** Narration says Order created or Quality check, but the visible rows show only At quality check (11.0 [06:40] [06:44]).

**Between 11.0 and 11.1**

- **On-time promise for shipments outside Saudi Arabia.** 11.0 says shipment within 2 business days everywhere else (11.0 [15:00]). 11.1 narration says shipment under 3 working days outside Saudi Arabia (11.1 [02:17]). The 11.1 legend reads AE (<= 2) and ZA (<= 2) for shipment (11.1 [02:31]). The pages do not agree on the limit for AE and ZA.
- **On-time denominator.** 11.0 says both measures are over decided orders (11.0 [14:57]). 11.1 says the outside-Saudi shipment denominator is shipments that happened (11.1 [02:17]).
- **Scoring weights.** The KAM Performance weights (defect rate 30%, days to solve 10%) differ from the seller next score weights (defect rate 60%, days to solve 20%, claims coverage 10%) (11.0 [12:06]; 11.1 [05:26]). These are separate tables. Teach them as separate, not as one scoring scheme.
- **Days to solve.** 11.0 defines it as the average days sellers take to resolve a claim, lower is better (11.0 [18:32] [18:37]). 11.1 adds calendar days, keyed on the repair partner, with in-progress claims excluded (11.1 [05:06]). Both can be true, but the 11.0 page does not state these details.

**Within 11.1**

- **Defect rate counting rule.** The Part 1 methodology says two claims on the same item count once (11.1 [03:03]). The Analysis tab says two claims on the same order count as one defect (11.1 [08:05]). These are different rules and the page does not settle which applies. Do not use this as a settled quiz answer.
- **On-time legend and rows.** The chart legend gives AE (<= 2) and ZA (<= 2), but shipment rows for ZA-74346-1 and 102740-3 show SLA "< 3" (11.1 [06:03] [07:47]). No single AE or ZA limit is stated as fact.
- **Row matching.** The trainer says 67% is higher than 65 and so the row is taken, but the screen shows -10% score impact. The rule for matching a KPI to a row is not stated (11.1 [05:45] [05:47] [05:54]).
- **Possible table error.** The Days to solve column in the scoring table reads 8 at +3%, 6 at +4% and 8 at +5%. The +4% value breaks the trend (11.1 [05:26]). This may be a misread.
- **Compare basis.** The KPI tiles compare with the previous period of the chosen timeframe (vs Jun 26 for a month, vs Aug 17 for a week, vs Sep 2 for a day). The narration says "the months before" (11.1 [01:23] [01:51]).

**Out of date or snapshot-dependent**

- The 11.0 refund snapshot is dated 2026-10-16, and its footers are about six days before the check date (11.0 Possibly out of date).
- The 11.0 counts (late 259, stuck 1,423, unidentified 979, refund 404, claims 1,235, sellers 149) are recording-time snapshots (11.0 Possibly out of date).
- The 11.1 demo figures are for June to August data, and the Sep* month is still running. Fixed periods such as 17 to 23 Aug are examples, not a current score (11.1 [05:01] [05:26]).
- The 11.1 "still filling", "no data" and "not settled yet" labels on tiles are not explained in the narration. Do not give them a meaning in teaching (11.1 [00:48] [01:27]).

**Three best scenario seeds (11.0):**

1. **Saudi return at 8 policy days.** It sits in the warning band, and the policy is applied at 9. Push the seller on it today (11.0 [09:16] [09:23] [09:32]).
