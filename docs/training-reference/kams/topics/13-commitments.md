# 13. Commitments

## What this topic covers

How a seller enters a commitment on the Commitments tab of the Seller Performance page: an offer to supply a quantity of a device at a fixed price for the commitment window, 20 Oct 2026 to 10 Nov 2026 (13.0 [00:07 t00007]). It covers the submission deadline and countdown, entering a price and quantity per device, the optional colour split, saving and changing an offer, and why commitments matter (13.0 [00:07 t00007] [00:12 t00012] [00:41] [00:43] [00:49 t00049]). The dates and figures come from a recording and may have changed (see Contradictions).

## How it works

1. Open the seller performance dashboard and go to the Commitments tab (13.0 [00:05] [00:07 t00007]).
2. Check the countdown box at the top right of the Commitments section, labelled SUBMISSIONS CLOSE IN. Submissions close Thursday 22 Oct 2026 at 6 PM UAE time (13.0 [00:10] [00:12 t00012]).
3. Note the time zone (UAE time) and the two tables. The recording shows only the UAE & South Africa table; the narration also names a Saudi Arabia table (13.0 [00:17] [00:18] [00:20 t00020]).
4. Read the suggested price and suggested quantity for each device. These are Revibe's proposal (13.0 [00:23 t00023] [00:24]).
5. On each device row you want to commit to, either type your own price (AED) and quantity, or click Use suggested. Leave the devices you do not want to commit to empty (13.0 [00:28] [00:32 t00032] [00:33]).
6. Optional: click Split by colour (optional) on a device row and enter colour quantities with SKUs. The colour quantities must add up exactly to the device quantity (13.0 [00:36] [00:38] [00:39 t00039]).
7. Check the bottom bar, which shows the number of devices, units and the AED total, plus the status (Nothing saved yet, Unsaved changes, or Saved ✓ with the time) (13.0 [00:28 t00032] [00:45 t00045]).
8. Click Save my offer in the bottom bar. The bar then shows Saved ✓ with the time, and the button is greyed out (13.0 [00:41] [00:45 t00045]).
9. Come back and change the offer as often as needed until submissions close. Each saved version is kept and listed in the saved versions panel, with the Current version marked (13.0 [00:43] [00:46] [00:45 t00045]).
10. Submit your offer before Thu 22 Oct 2026, 6 PM UAE time (13.0 [00:54] [00:57] [01:00 t00060]).
11. After submissions close, Revibe reviews the offers and confirms the accepted commitments with the seller (13.0 [00:07 t00007]).
12. Accepted commitments are locked in the buybox at the seller's price for the whole window, until the committed quantity is sold (13.0 [00:48] [00:53] [00:49 t00049]).
