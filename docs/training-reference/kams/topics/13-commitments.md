## What this topic covers

This topic covers the Commitments tab in the Seller Performance dashboard (13.0 [00:05] [00:07 t00007]). A commitment means the seller commits to supply a quantity of a device at a fixed price for the whole commitment window (13.0 [00:07 t00007] [00:28]). The video shows one round: the window runs from 20 Oct 2026 to 10 Nov 2026, and submissions close on Thu 22 Oct at 18:00 UAE time (13.0 [00:00] [00:07 t00007] [00:12] [00:17]).

It explains how to fill in an offer: use the suggested price and quantity or type your own, leave unwanted devices empty, and optionally split a quantity by colour. It then covers how to save and change the offer, and what happens after the close. Revibe reviews the offers and confirms the accepted ones, which are then locked in the buybox at the seller's price (13.0 [00:24] [00:28] [00:33] [00:36] [00:41] [00:43] [00:48] [00:07 t00007]).

## How it works

1. **Know the round.** Commitments lock in the seller's price and volume for a window. This round runs from 20 Oct 2026 to 10 Nov 2026 (13.0 [00:00] [00:07 t00007]).
2. **Open the Commitments tab.** Go to the Seller Performance dashboard, then the "new" Commitments tab. It sits between Price recommendations and Payment (13.0 [00:05] [00:07 t00007]). The clicks to get there are not shown. The video cuts straight to the tab, already selected (13.0 [00:05] [00:07 t00007]).
3. **Check the countdown.** The "SUBMISSIONS CLOSE IN" box at the top right shows the time left. It also shows when submissions close: Thu 22 Oct, 18:00 (UAE time) (13.0 [00:10] [00:12] [00:17] [00:12 t00012]).
4. **Find the right table.** There are two tables, one for UAE & South Africa and one for Saudi Arabia (13.0 [00:18]). Only the UAE & South Africa table is shown ("19 devices open"). The Saudi Arabia table is mentioned but never appears (13.0 [00:18] [00:20 t00020]). The columns are DEVICE, SUGGESTED PRICE, SUGGESTED QUANTITY, YOUR PRICE (AED) and YOUR QUANTITY (13.0 [00:20 t00020]).
5. **Read the suggestions.** Revibe suggests a price and a quantity for each device. For example, iphone-12 · 64 GB · Good has a suggested 495 / 245 (13.0 [00:24] [00:20 t00020]).
6. **Fill in the devices you want.** For each device, type the price and quantity you can commit to for the whole window, or click "Use suggested" (13.0 [00:28]). Prices are in AED (13.0 [00:07 t00007] [00:20 t00020]). In the demo, the seller typed 480 / 200 on iphone-12 · 64 GB · Good, and the bar showed AED 96,000 (13.0 [00:28] [00:32 t00032]). iphone-14 · 128 GB · Good was then filled with its suggested 865 / 225, most likely with "Use suggested". The click itself falls between frames (13.0 [00:33] [00:32 t00032] [00:34 t00034]).
7. **Leave the rest empty.** Devices you don't want stay empty. Only devices with a price and a quantity count in the offer (13.0 [00:33] [00:07 t00007] [00:32 t00032]). The bottom bar keeps a running total, price × quantity summed over devices. In the demo it read "Your offer: 2 devices, 425 units, AED 290,625" (13.0 [00:34 t00034]).
8. **Optionally, split by colour.** "▸ Split by colour (optional)" lists each colour with its own SKU (13.0 [00:36] [00:39 t00039]). If you split, the colours must add up to your quantity (13.0 [00:38]). In the demo, the seller entered Black 120 against 200 units, and several things happened:
   - the field turned red;
   - the error "Colours add up to 120, not 200." appeared;
   - the counter showed a red "120 / 200";
   - the bar showed "1 line to fix";
   - the device dropped out of the totals, which became 1 device, 225 units (13.0 [00:39 t00039]).

   Once the colours added up (Black 120, White 50, Purple 30 = a green "200 / 200"), the device counted again (13.0 [00:41] [00:43 t00043]). The Dell laptop row shows no colour-split link, only "Use suggested" (13.0 [00:20 t00020]).
9. **Save.** Click "Save my offer" (13.0 [00:41]). The bar's status shows "Nothing saved yet." before the first save and "Unsaved changes" while changes are pending (13.0 [00:07 t00007] [00:32 t00032] [00:43 t00043]). After saving, it shows "Saved ✓ <date, time>" and "Last saved … by <email> · <n> version" (13.0 [00:45 t00045]).
10. **Change it until the deadline.** The seller can come back and change the offer as often as they like until submissions close (13.0 [00:43] [00:07 t00007]). Every version is kept in the "Saved versions" list. Each version shows its date and time, devices, units, AED total and who saved it (13.0 [00:46] [00:45 t00045]).
11. **Revibe reviews after the close.** After submissions close, Revibe reviews the offers and confirms the accepted commitments with the seller (13.0 [00:07 t00007]). Saving an offer is not the same as having it accepted.
12. **Accepted commitments are locked in the buybox.** They stay in the buybox at the seller's committed price for the whole window, until the committed quantity is sold (13.0 [00:48] [00:53] [00:49 t00049]). The benefit ("Predictable volume") is that the seller can buy stock in advance with confidence (13.0 [00:54] [00:49 t00049]).
