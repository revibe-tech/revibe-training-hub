# 1. Onboarding App

Sources: item 1.0 (KAM, 15:24), item 1.1 (Seller, about 14:39), item 1.2 (Seller, Fulfillment, 10:50). Citations use the item id and the video time, for example (1.0 [03:12]). Frame references (tNNNNN) are omitted here; see the item pages.

## What this topic covers

- The seller onboarding process from the public website form to a fully onboarded seller.
- The Seller Onboarding App (AppSheet): the form sellers fill in, the seller agreement and quality guidelines steps, and the status and approval outcomes.
- The admin side the KAM uses to review applications: Profile list, Approval, Rationale, Onboarding Plan (outbound recruiting), Leads, and the Processed applications sub-statuses.
- The first Fulfillment App shipment for quality screening (1.2), which follows directly after acceptance.

## How it works

Consolidated in process order. Stage names follow item 1.1 and 1.2 (four stages); item 1.0 frames the same process as Inbound, Outbound, Seller view and Admin view.

1. **Entry point (inbound).** Most sellers find onboarding on the Revibe website: For Businesses has a Become a seller button, and the footer has Become A Seller under About Us (1.0 [01:01]; 1.0 [01:14]; 1.0 [01:17]). The public page is "Become Official Revibe Seller" (1.0 [01:28]).
2. **Public form.** The seller enters first name, last name, email, phone, business name, country, units sold per month and uploads a trade licence (1.0 [01:28]). On submit the seller is redirected to the AppSheet app "Revibe - Seller Onboarding App" (1.0 [01:47]). The app warns that onboarding may take a few weeks (1.0 [02:03]). One-time sellers are sent to sell.revibe.me instead (1.0 [02:11]; 1.1 [04:45]).
3. **Introduction video and Users Form.** The seller watches the introduction video and clicks Ok (1.0 [02:24]; 1.1 [04:52]). The Users Form (side panel) asks for company and contact details. Email is pre-filled (1.0 [02:37]; 1.1 [05:11]). Company name is "as mentioned in your trading licence" (1.0 [02:37]; 1.1 [05:04]).
4. **Address and location.** Country set to UAE reveals an Emirate field (1.0 [02:52]). The seller gives the exact address they ship orders from, and the exact Google Maps location, copied via Share and pasted into the field (1.0 [02:48]; 1.1 [05:25]; 1.1 [06:01]).
5. **Sales and operations questions.** Products sold (multi-select), wholesaler (B2B) Yes/No, sells online Yes/No (Yes opens the required platform question, Amazon or Noon, with a seller page URL), units per month, grades, as is or refurbished, technicians, daily shipping capacity after quality check, how they heard about Revibe, and whether they know anyone who sells at Revibe (1.0 [03:15] to [04:00]; 1.1 [06:08] to [07:29]).
6. **Optional text.** "Would you like to add something?" is not mandatory (1.0 [04:09]; 1.1 [07:30]).
7. **Seller agreement.** The seller opens the agreement link, reads it, signs it, and uploads the signed file in the field "Please upload here the seller agreement once signed". The status goes from To do to Done (1.0 [04:18] to [05:03]; 1.1 [07:37] to [08:56]). Item 1.0 shows a DocuSign PowerForm; item 1.1 shows a Google Doc. See Contradictions.
8. **Quality guidelines.** The seller opens the guidelines in another tab and answers "Have you read the quality guidelines?" with Yes (1.0 [05:20] to [05:29]; 1.1 [09:16] to [10:58]). The Yes answer reveals the legal documentation section (1.0 [05:35]).
9. **Legal documentation.** Tax registration certificate, trading licence, trading licence number, Emirates ID, position in the company (CEO / Founder, Manager, Other, etc.), bank details, and "Do you have a DHL account?" (1.0 [05:36] to [06:06]; 1.1 [11:01] to [11:45]). Yes to DHL reveals the account number field for Revibe's preferential DHL rates (1.0 [06:06]; 1.1 [11:10]).
10. **Save and status.** Save submits the application. Status moves to Under review (1.0 [06:11]; 1.1 [11:59]).
11. **Admin review (KAM).** Profile list, Under Review tab. Check the seller's details, especially the address and the Noon or Amazon seller page, then the documents: contract, tax registration certificate, trading licence, Emirates ID, bank details (1.0 [06:32] to [07:00]). Set Approval (Incomplete, Final review shown, Accepted or Rejected per the narration). Incomplete moves Application status to Missing data and opens a Rationale checklist (1.0 [07:19] to [07:24]). Attach proof and add an internal comment when needed (1.0 [07:29]).
12. **Seller sees the outcome (1.1).** Profile list groups: Under review, Incomplete, Accepted. Incomplete shows a Rationale such as "address is not precise" or bank details to be updated. The seller clicks Edit, fixes the cleared fields, and saves. The status returns to Under review (1.1 [12:14] to [13:00]).
13. **Accepted.** The Next steps box shows "Congrats!", says the profile is validated, that access to the fulfillment app follows, that a first batch of devices must be sent, and asks the seller to watch the video (1.0 [14:17] to [14:39]; 1.1 [13:28] to [13:49]). The seller sets "Have you watched the Fulfillment App video?" to Yes and saves. The Link to Fulfillment App then appears and opens the Fulfillment App (1.1 [14:01] to [14:20]; 1.2 [00:44] to [01:14]).
14. **Quality screening (stage 2, 1.2).** Inside the Fulfillment App the seller browses SKUs available, builds a shipment, adds request lines with Quantity Sent and Price, saves, and prepares the box with a tracking label (1.2 [01:14] to [08:26]). Revibe inspects the first shipment. A pass leads to Sales + Onboarding, a fail to Return + No-onboarding (1.1 [03:03] to [03:21]; 1.2 [00:26] to [00:44]).
15. **Trial period and full onboarding (1.1).** Trial period: only Fulfillment by Revibe is available, the seller sends batches, and gets access to Revibe tools and trainings (1.1 [03:33] to [03:54]). Full onboarding: once a significant volume of orders is reached, direct shipping is allowed with no limits (1.1 [04:06] to [04:13]).
16. **Outbound and leads (KAM only, 1.0).** The KAM works an Onboarding Plan sheet of outbound sellers (1.0 [07:52] to [09:00]), and works leads: sellers who completed the website form but did not finish the app (1.0 [10:16] to [11:41]). Accepted sub-statuses run in this order: Access to create, Pending, Postponed, Requested, Received, Second chance, Rejected, Onboarded (1.0 [12:30]). The KAM's mission for an assigned seller is to connect and drive them through onboarding (1.0 [13:57] to [14:03]).

## Contradictions

- **Approval visibility.** Item 1.0 says the Approval field is admin-only (1.0 [06:17]; flagged unconfirmed). Item 1.1 shows the seller seeing Approval and Rationale in their own profile (1.1 [12:14]; 1.1 [12:31]).
- **Agreement format.** Item 1.0 shows a DocuSign PowerForm where the seller signs, then uploads the signed copy (1.0 [04:22] to [04:45]). Item 1.1 shows a Google Doc agreement that the seller completes, downloads as PDF and uploads (1.1 [07:52] to [08:50]). Both are titled "Revibe Seller Agreement - March 2025". The current process is not stated.
- **Quality check numbers.** Item 1.0 reads "Send 20/30 pieces to our lab" (1.0 [00:00 p5]; unclear). Item 1.1 says the first shipment is "between 20 and 30 products" only as a low-confidence example (1.1 [02:56]). Item 1.2 recommends at least 30 to 50 pieces, spoken only (1.2 [07:59]; 1.2 [08:05]).
- **Quality check definition.** Item 1.1 gives quality screening as the first shipment inspected against guidelines (1.1 [02:29]). Its glossary also defines "Quality check" as checking a device when an order is received (1.1 [06:55]; medium confidence). These are different things.
- **Warranty.** Item 1.1 gives 6 months (1.1 [01:22]). Item 1.0 notes the speech says one year and the seller supports six months, while the slide shows six months (1.0 unclear, 01:13 and 01:22). Treat the 6-month figure as the one on screen.
- **Countries.** Item 1.1 says Revibe operates in three countries, with KSA shipping done by the seller (1.1 [00:25]; 1.1 [00:32]). Item 1.0 says "more than 300 sellers across the UAE" while the Onboarding Plan sheet also shows KSA (1.0 [07:55]; flagged unclear).
- **Stage framing.** Items 1.1 and 1.2 use four stages (Profile screening, Quality screening, Trial period, Full onboarding). Item 1.0 uses a Sellers' journey slide with Onboarding App, Quality check, then Onboarded or Rejected (1.0 [00:00 p5]). The slide in 1.0 is footed "2024" (1.0 possibly out of date).
- **Onboarded timing.** Item 1.0 says the seller is onboarded once onboarding is done, and the slides place an ONBOARDING tag above step 3 (1.0 [03:27]; flagged unclear). Item 1.1 says full onboarding is stage 4 (1.1 [04:06]).
- **Quality guidelines location.** Item 1.2 says the trainer reads them "on the onboarding app", while the screen shows a separate Preview PDF (1.2 [09:53]; flagged). Item 1.1 says the guidelines are a Drive link in the form (1.1 [08:58]). Item 1.0 says the guidelines are opened in another tab (1.0 [05:20]). The guidelines are also linked from two different Drive links (1.0 possibly out of date).
- **Camera dot limits.** Item 1.1 gives Very good as 1 small dot on one row and 2 on another (1.1 [09:22]; 1.1 [09:33]). Item 1.2 notes the same conflict (1.2 [09:56]).
- **Sticker placement.** Item 1.2 speech says a sticker on every device, while the slide says a sticker on the bubble wrap (1.2 [08:49]; flagged unclear).
- **Airway bill vs tracking label.** Item 1.2 says an "airway bill" is created an hour after the request (1.2 [06:25]). It also says a "tracking label link" appears after an hour (1.2 [08:18]). The sentence about the airway bill is cut off (1.2 [09:03]). Unresolved.

## Quiz blueprint

Suggested counts follow the rule of about one question per minute of video, at most 20 per video. For each page: 1.0 is about 15 minutes, 1.1 about 14.6 minutes, 1.2 about 11 minutes.

### 1.0 Onboarding App (KAM), about 15 questions

- **Areas to cover:** the public form and required fields; the seller agreement and legal documentation; the admin review and Approval outcomes; the Rationale for Incomplete; Onboarding Plan tracking; lead stages and call steps; the Accepted sub-statuses and their order; Profile groups.
- **Multiple-answer suitable:** the required fields on the public form (1.0 [01:28]); legal documentation fields (1.0 [05:36]); documents to check during review (1.0 [06:54]); Rationale options when Approval is Incomplete (1.0 [07:24]); Onboarding Plan tracking columns (1.0 [08:31]).
- **Single-answer suitable:** the four onboarding parts; lead stage flow; Accepted sub-status order; Pending meaning; Onboarded meaning.

Scenario seeds:

1. A seller says they only want to sell a few items once. The correct action is to direct them to sell.revibe.me, not the onboarding app (1.0 [02:11]; 1.0 [02:06]).
2. A reviewer finds a missing address and bank detail. The correct action is to set Approval to Incomplete, which sets Application status to Missing data, then pick the missing element in Rationale and add a comment (1.0 [07:19]; 1.0 [07:22]; 1.0 [07:24]).
3. A lead says they are still interested after a call. The correct action is to send the onboarding app link, set the Stage to Sent to app, and record the call in the Comment field (1.0 [11:03]; 1.0 [11:21]; 1.0 [11:28]).

### 1.1 Onboarding App (Seller), about 15 questions

- **Areas to cover:** the four stages and what each means; the form fields in order; the seller agreement steps; the Approval outcomes and how to fix an Incomplete profile; the Next steps after acceptance and how to reach the Fulfillment App; the quality guideline grade rules; agreement clauses on returns, penalties and termination; the DHL rule.

### 1.2 Onboarding App Fulfillment (Seller), about 11 questions

- **Areas to cover:** the steps to get the Fulfillment App link; the SKUs available list; creating a shipment and adding lines; the price rule; packing and cables; tracking and pickup; the quality rules that apply to devices.

- **Single-answer suitable:** the price rule; only one shipment open at a time; one USB-C cable per device; the 9,999 price meaning; the rule that unrequested models are shipped back.

Scenario seeds:

2. A seller enters 598 as the price for the example SKU, where the Buybox price is 598. The error says to beat Buybox by at least 1 AED, so set 597 (1.2 [03:37]; 1.2 [03:42]; 1.2 [03:47]).
3. A seller wants to add a model that was never requested on the app. It must be requested on the app first; otherwise it is shipped back immediately (1.2 [08:39]; 1.2 [08:42]).
