# 6. Returns

## What this topic covers

How a customer return claim is handled once it reaches the seller: the return eligibility rules, the four-step Revibe Return Process, the seller's Mark valid and Mark invalid decisions, the invalid-claim proof route, the Under revision stage, and the Revibe Lab final-decision outcomes. Also covers where to find claims in the admin portal and what the claim panel shows (6.0 [00:00 t00000], [00:19], [01:33], [03:00 t00180], [04:47 t00288]).

Source pages: 6.0 (Seller) only.

## How it works

One consolidated walkthrough, in process order, from 6.0 only.

1. **Eligibility.** A customer can return a device within 15 days after delivery. A second valid claim on the same order is accepted even after that period (6.0 [00:19], [00:25], [00:33]).
2. **Step 1, customer raises the claim.** The customer raises the claim through My Account on Revibe. The status is Pending (Revibe team to review). No seller action at this step, and the seller is not contacted yet (6.0 [00:40 t00040], [00:46]).
3. **Step 2, Revibe Agent Check.** The Revibe team reviews the customer's proof. If it is relevant, the request is validated and marked Completed, with status Pending Collection. No seller action (6.0 [01:00 t00060], [00:49]).
4. **Step 3, pickup airway bill (AWB).** For UAE and South Africa orders (AE and ZA on the slide), the AWB is created automatically and the device is collected and delivered to the seller. For Saudi Arabia orders (SA on the slide), the seller creates the AWB manually so the device is collected and delivered to the seller (6.0 [01:09], [01:20 t00080], [01:27]).
5. **Step 4, seller action.** Once the courier delivers the device to the seller, the claim moves to Under QC in the seller portal. The seller then chooses Mark valid or Mark invalid (6.0 [01:33 t00100], [01:40 t00100], [01:45]).
6. **Mark valid.** The customer is refunded immediately and the case is closed (instant refund) (6.0 [01:50], [02:00 t00120], [06:13 t00379]).
7. **Mark invalid.** The seller must submit proof: a written message plus supporting proof, with attachments under 100 MB. The Invalid claim comment and the supplier proof are recorded on the claim (6.0 [02:03 t00140], [06:20 t00397], [06:37 t00397], [06:38 t00398], [06:44 t00404]).
8. **Revibe expert check of the proof.** Revibe's expert team checks the seller's proof. If it is not relevant, the seller is asked again to mark the claim valid or invalid. If good enough, the device is sent to the Revibe Lab for a final decision (6.0 [02:21], [02:27], [02:34], [02:40 t00160], [02:44]).
9. **Revibe confirmation.** Revibe records its own decision, Valid claim or Invalid claim, with a comment in the Revibe confirmation fields. If Revibe sets Invalid, the claim and device go to the Lab. If Revibe sets Valid, the claim returns to the seller at Under revision (6.0 [07:23], [07:33], [07:41 t00461], [07:43], [07:52], [08:21 t00497]).
10. **Under revision (seller's second decision).** The claim shows Valid claim as Not Replied, and the seller chooses Valid claim, Invalid claim or Not replied (6.0 [08:17 t00497], [08:28], [08:38 t00518]).
    - **Valid at this stage:** the customer is fully refunded and the seller keeps the device (6.0 [08:43], [08:52 t00523]).
    - **Invalid at this stage:** the seller edits the claim, marks it Invalid, removes the previous proof, provides a new proof and saves (6.0 [09:19 t00559], [09:28]). The save itself is not shown on screen.
11. **Revibe confirms again and sends to the Lab.** Revibe sets its confirmation to Invalid claim, adds a comment (the trainer's example: "proof relevant, send to Revibe Lab") and the device is sent automatically to Revibe Lab. The case is then closed for the seller (6.0 [09:36], [09:43], [09:51], [09:57], [10:09 t00602]). The save and the automatic transfer are not shown on screen.
12. **LAB decision.** A Valid LAB decision triggers an "Are you sure?" dialog (Confirm or Cancel) and the customer is refunded with no possibility to go back (6.0 [10:27 t00627], [10:30], [10:34 t00634]).
    - **LAB Valid:** full refund to the customer and the device is shipped back to the seller (6.0 [02:52], [03:01 t00180]).
    - **LAB Invalid:** the customer pays and the device is shipped to the customer. If the customer does not pay, Revibe keeps the device (6.0 [03:02], [03:09], [03:13 t00180], [10:19], [10:25]).
13. **Time limits (spoken, not on the PDF page 1 frames).** After six working days from receipt of the device, Revibe may issue a full refund to the customer if there is no seller cooperation. After eight working days, even with cooperation, Revibe may issue a full refund (6.0 [11:35], [11:40], [11:46], [11:52], [11:58]).

Portal navigation for the seller: admin.revibe.me, then Claim Management, then R - Seller Returns. Click the claim row to open the side panel with Mark valid, Mark invalid and Order details (6.0 [04:34 t00273], [04:41], [04:47 t00288], [05:26], [05:30 t00330]).

## Contradictions

Only one page is in the pack, so there are no cross-page conflicts. The following conflicts and gaps are within 6.0. None is flagged as out of date (the page records no outdated items).

- **Working-day deadlines.** The spoken reminders give five (low-confidence), six and eight working days (6.0 [11:35], [11:40], [11:52]). None appears on screen; the PDF page 1 frames show no deadline text (6.0 [11:32 t00692]). These need checking against the source PDF before use.
- **Step 2 status.** The spoken and slide content says the request is marked Completed after validation, while the label beside it reads Pending Collection. The screen does not say which applies (6.0 [01:00 t00060], [12:12 t00732]).
- **Ship-Back label.** The slide footer labels Ship-Back "sent to customer", the portal calls the block Shipback Tracking Information, and the Lab valid outcome sends the device back to the seller. Which party the block refers to is unclear (6.0 [00:00 t00000], [05:48 t00340], [03:01 t00180]).
- **Battery threshold.** The rules say no threshold is given on screen, yet the seller comment field shows "Battery health checked and above 95%". The page treats 95% as a sample comment, not a rule (6.0 [04:05 t00242], [06:38 t00398]).
- **Invalid button after submit.** The trainer says Mark invalid is "not available anymore" after invalid proof is submitted. Frame 06:44 does not clearly show it disabled, so the claim is unconfirmed and is not recorded as a step (6.0 [06:45], [06:51], [06:44 t00404]).
- **Mark VALID proof.** The page states that Mark valid does not need proof, but this is implied only (6.0 [02:03 t00120]).
- **Under revision Valid versus Lab Valid.** At Under revision, Valid means the seller keeps the device (6.0 [08:52 t00523]). Lab Valid means the device is shipped back to the seller (6.0 [03:01 t00180]). Both are stated; the page does not explain how they relate.
- **Country codes.** AE and ZA, and SA, are shown on screen without expansion. The UAE, South Africa and Saudi Arabia names come from speech only, and the mapping is not confirmed for each code (6.0 [01:09], [10:51], [10:58], [12:12 t00732]).
