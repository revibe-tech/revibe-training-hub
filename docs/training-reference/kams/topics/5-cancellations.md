# 5. Cancellations

Source pages: [5.0 Cancellations (Seller)](../items/5.0.md), a 3:23 video for sellers.

## What this topic covers

This topic covers the customer cancellation policy that Revibe had "just launched" when the video was recorded, around May 2025 (5.0 [00:00] [00:44 t00044]). It explains why customers cancel, how to recognise a cancelled order, and how to open its cancellation claim (5.0 [00:14] [00:36 t00036] [00:44 t00044] [00:56 t00056]). It then covers how to accept the cancellation (Mark valid) or reject it (Mark Invalid), and the 24-hour and country/stage rules that decide which one is allowed (5.0 [01:00] [01:04] [01:09] [01:31] [01:53] [02:01]).

A note on the source: the trainer is a French speaker, and speech recognition misheard some of his accented English. This page uses the corrected readings from the item page. For example, "on your site" is read as "on your side" (5.0 [00:24]). The one line whose meaning stays unclear, about which countries need a tracking ID to reject, is explained under Contradictions (5.0 [02:22]).

The demo was recorded on an admin dashboard (stage-admin.revibe.me), so a seller's own screens may look different (5.0 [00:00 t00000]).

## How it works

1. **The customer cancels.** A customer cancels for one of two reasons: they changed their mind and no longer want the device, or the shipment is delayed on the seller's side and they don't want to wait any longer (5.0 [00:14] [00:20] [00:24]).
2. **Spot the cancelled order.** In the orders list, a cancelled order has a small purple bar on the left edge of its row. The trainer calls it a "small purple banner" (5.0 [00:36] [00:36 t00036]).
3. **Open the order.** A purple banner at the top of the order panel says the order is on hold and how long ago. In the demo it read "This order is currently on hold (about 1 hour ago)." (5.0 [00:44] [00:44 t00044]). While the order is on hold, the seller cannot edit it or do anything else with it, and must first respond to the claim that was created (5.0 [00:44] [00:51]).
4. **Find the claim.** The cancellation appears in the Claims section of the Order Details tab, with Type 'Cancellation' and Stage 'To be cancelled'. In the demo the Subtype was 'Change of mind' (5.0 [00:44 t00044] [00:51]).
5. **Open the Claim Details tab.** Click the Claim ID link in the Claims section. A 'Claim Details' tab opens next to Order Details, with the buttons Mark valid and Mark Invalid (5.0 [00:56] [00:56 t00056] [01:16 t00076]). The admin demo also showed Mark Refunded and Cancel override buttons. The trainer did not explain them, and a seller's own view may not show them (5.0 [01:16 t00076] [00:00 t00000]).
6. **Respond within 24 hours.** If the seller does not respond within 24 hours after the order was cancelled, Revibe treats the cancellation as valid: the order is cancelled and the customer is refunded (5.0 [01:09] [01:16] [01:22]).
7. **Check the country and stage.** These decide whether the seller may reject:

   | Country | Order stage | What the seller can do | Source |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | UAE, South Africa | Created | Must always accept (Mark valid) | 5.0 [01:31] [01:38] |
   | UAE, South Africa | Quality check | May reject (Mark Invalid), then must ship within the next 24 hours | 5.0 [01:45] [01:53] [02:01] |
   | Saudi Arabia | Any (the trainer says "always") | May reject (Mark Invalid), then must ship within the next 24 hours | 5.0 [02:01] [02:07] [02:15] |

   Other stages for UAE and South Africa are not covered (5.0 [01:31]).
8. **To accept, click Mark valid.** The order is cancelled and the customer is refunded (5.0 [01:00] [01:22] [01:31]).
9. **To reject, click Mark Invalid and add a tracking ID if asked.** The trainer says rejecting needs a tracking ID (5.0 [02:15]). In the demo, Mark Invalid on a UAE order with no tracking ID showed the error "Tracking ID is required to mark the claim as invalid" and opened an 'Add tracking ID' pop-up with a Tracking ID field and Cancel / Add buttons (5.0 [02:19 t00139] [00:44 t00044] [00:00 t00000]). (Unconfirmed) If the tracking ID is already on the order, the seller can just press Mark Invalid (5.0 [02:22] [02:31] [02:38]). Which countries need the tracking ID is ambiguous; see Contradictions.
10. **Ship within 24 hours.** Rejecting commits the seller to shipping the order within the next 24 hours (5.0 [01:53] [02:01] [02:07]). What happens if they don't ship in time is not covered (5.0 [02:01]).
11. **(Unconfirmed) The order goes back to normal.** The trainer says that once the claim is marked invalid, the order appears again as a normal order in the orders dashboard and can be edited and handled as usual. This was said but not shown: the demo never finished the rejection, and at the end the claim was still Open and the row still had its purple bar (5.0 [02:40] [02:48] [03:22 t00202]).

## Contradictions

**Between pages:** none found. There is only one page.

**With anything flagged out of date:** the trainer calls this "the new cancellation policy that we just launched", and the demo claim is dated 2025-05-19. Check that the 24-hour rules and the country/stage rules still apply (5.0 [00:00] [00:44 t00044]).

**Within the page:**

- **Which countries need a tracking ID to reject (5.0 [02:22]).** The line "This you will have to do it for Saudi Arabia orders only, for UAE and South Africa orders." contradicts itself, and the trainer's French phrasing makes it hard to read. One possible reading, based on his phrasing and pauses, is: "You have to input a tracking ID for Saudi Arabia orders only. For UAE and South Africa orders, if the tracking ID is already here you can just press invalid." Other readings are that all three countries need it, or that UAE and South Africa do not. On screen, a UAE order with no tracking ID did get the "Tracking ID is required" error (5.0 [02:19 t00139] [00:44 t00044] [00:00 t00000]). The next line, "you won't need to put tracking ID link", is also garbled; "link" may be a mishearing (5.0 [02:38]). **Confirm with the trainer before teaching or testing this.**
- **On hold but an Edit button shows (5.0 [00:44]).** The trainer says an on-hold order cannot be edited, yet the order panel header shows an 'Edit' button while the on-hold banner is displayed (5.0 [00:44 t00044] [00:52 t00052]). This may be because the demo is in the admin view.
- **Said vs shown after rejection (5.0 [02:40]).** The trainer says a rejected order goes back to normal, but the demo ends with the claim still Open and the purple bar still on the row, because the rejection was never completed on screen (5.0 [02:40] [02:48] [03:22 t00202]).

- "Why do customers cancel?" Correct: change of mind, shipment delayed on the seller's side (5.0 [00:20] [00:24]).

**3 best scenario seeds:**
