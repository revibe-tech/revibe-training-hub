# 4. Orders App

## What this topic covers

How sellers use the Orders App on the Revibe admin site (admin.revibe.me): reading the Orders list and its stage groups, opening and editing an order, confirming availability, the airway bill (UAE and South Africa) and tracking ID (Saudi Arabia) steps, the 48-hour shipping rule and how courier scan data decides late orders, the Request Revibe box, Invoices and the Payment Tool (4.0 [00:00] [00:04] [11:09] [11:18]).

Source: one video, 4.0 Orders App (TBU) (Seller), 727 s (12:07). The item is flagged TBU (to be updated) (4.0 [00:00]), so every screen, rule and number needs checking before use.

## How it works

One walkthrough in the order the trainer presents it. Screens are on admin.revibe.me.

1. **Open the app.** The left menu has Dashboard, Order Management, Invoices and Payment Tool. Order Management is the orders app (4.0 [00:21] [00:25]).
2. **Read the stage groups.** The Order List is split into stage groups: 02 Order created, 03 At quality check, 04 Shipped, 05 Delivered (4.0 [00:28] [00:41] [00:48] [00:57]).
3. **Read a row.** Order Help holds the order reference (for example 61021-1) and the working hours since the order was created (4.0 [01:01] [01:08]). Other columns include the request, model, variation, delivery method, COD to collect, confirmed date, category, tracking company, actual cost, payment method, active or closed, and SKU (4.0 [01:14] [01:24] [01:31] [01:38] [01:54] [02:09]).
4. **Identify the country.** UAE orders have no prefix. ZA means South Africa. SA means Saudi Arabia (4.0 [02:40] [02:46] [02:49] [02:54]).
5. **Open the side panel.** Click an order row. The panel shows the order status and tracking information. Edit is at the top right and View Details at the bottom (4.0 [02:02] [02:09] [02:16] [02:23]).
6. **Edit.** Click Edit; Save and Cancel then appear (4.0 [02:16] [02:22]). The edit page is also reachable from the full order page (4.0 [02:30]).
7. **View details.** View Details opens the full order page (for order 61021-1, admin.revibe.me/dashboard/order/5/184824). It shows the shipment status, the Comments panel and the Order Timeline (4.0 [02:30] [03:03] [03:12] [03:18] [03:22]).
8. **Confirm availability (every order).** When an order is received, confirm availability first. Open the order, click Edit, set Availability Status under Order Status, then confirm (4.0 [04:29-04:33] [04:43-04:45] [05:54] [10:27-10:32]).
9. **Not Available (avoid if possible).** Choosing Not Available opens a confirmation dialog. The trainer says it charges 5% of cost (4.0 [04:55] [05:00-05:09]). See Contradictions.
10. **Move to quality check.** After availability is confirmed, the order moves to 03 At quality check (4.0 [05:54-06:00]). For UAE and South Africa orders, confirming availability is also the final step (4.0 [04:35-04:40]).
11. **Airway bill (UAE and South Africa).** Once an order is at At quality check, an airway bill is generated automatically. The trainer says the UAE bill is made by QuickUp and the South Africa bill by DHL (4.0 [06:02] [06:18]). For South Africa, open the airway bill from the Tracking Label Link, download it, put it on the shipment and wait for DHL (4.0 [06:25-06:45]).
12. **Hand over to DHL.** DHL must scan the shipment at the seller's location at pickup. Do not let DHL take it away to scan later (4.0 [06:48-07:00]).
13. **Ship within 48 hours.** The seller has 48 hours to ship the order (4.0 [07:04]).
14. **Saudi Arabia tracking ID.** For a Saudi order, click Edit and enter the Tracking ID under Tracking Information. The Tracking Label Link is optional (4.0 [07:24-07:53] [10:43-10:52]).
15. **Automatic status change.** Courier scan data moves the order from 03 At quality check to 04 Shipped automatically. The courier sends the scan time to Revibe, sometimes a few hours late (4.0 [08:04-08:27] [08:57-09:04]).
16. **Check the timing.** Full order page, Shipment Status Logs and Order Timeline show when the order was created, put at quality check and shipped (4.0 [08:32-08:48] [09:17]). An order shipped more than 48 hours after confirmation is late (4.0 [09:35-09:38]).
17. **Ask Revibe for help.** Open the order, click Edit, choose an option in the Request Revibe dropdown, add a comment, then Save (4.0 [10:58-11:06]). Revibe teams can only help if the request is in this box (4.0 [03:56-04:04]).
18. **Invoices and payments.** Open Invoices from the left menu and use Download on each row (4.0 [11:09-11:18]). The Payment Tool shows Total Payable and tracks past and upcoming payments; payments are made each time an order is shipped (trainer's statement) (4.0 [11:18] [11:24]).
19. **Score.** Order handling affects the seller score, which uses four KPIs (4.0 [11:35] [11:55-12:01]).

## Contradictions

- **Confirmation step.** The video says availability is confirmed as the first step for every order (4.0 [04:29-04:33] [10:27-10:32]), and says it is also the final step for UAE and South Africa orders (4.0 [04:35-04:40]). These do not conflict but are not spelled out as separate rules. At 10:27 the trainer says to confirm all orders, yet no frame shows the confirm action (4.0 [10:27-10:32]).
- **Not Available charge.** The trainer says the dialog shows an error message (4.0 [04:55]). The frame shows a confirmation dialog (4.0 [t00300]). The 5% figure is 45.1 on 902, but the example shows 46 (4.0 [05:02-05:09]). The currency word is garbled (4.0 [05:09]). Whether Cancel avoids the charge is not shown (4.0 [05:02]).
- **Cancellation charge.** The trainer gives a 5% charge on cancellation (4.0 [10:33-10:36]). The Not Available charge is also 5% (4.0 [05:00]). The video does not say if these are the same trigger, and the cost base is not stated (4.0 [10:33-10:36]).
- **Late-order definition.** The 48-hour rule is stated as shipping within 48 hours (4.0 [07:04]). The late-order example is "shipped more than 48 hours after being confirmed" (4.0 [09:35-09:38]). The trainer does not reconcile the two bases, and "working hours" is mentioned without an explanation (4.0 [07:09]).
- **Tracking ID for Saudi orders.** The trainer says Saudi orders need a tracking ID (4.0 [07:30]), and that it is entered once availability is confirmed and the order is at quality check (4.0 [07:35-07:45]). The Tracking Label Link is optional (4.0 [07:53]). The 10:43 wording says the ID comes from the delivery company (4.0 [10:43-10:52]).
- **Request Revibe options.** The Edit page shows four options (4.0 [03:37 t00217]). The dropdown shows six: Need Address, Need Phone Number, Other (Write Comment), Revibe Replied, String and Confirmed (4.0 [11:06 t00666]). The trainer names only three, and String and Confirmed are not explained.
- **South Africa airway bill.** The trainer says the link is copied and put in the field (4.0 [06:32-06:45]). It is not clear whether the link is copied from or pasted into the Tracking Label Link field (4.0 [06:32]).
- **Shipping date on the Order Timeline.** The trainer says the shipping date will always be accurate (4.0 [09:17]). He also says the courier can send the date late, and the Timeline shows Delivered as "-" while the logs show a Delivered entry (4.0 [09:56-10:19] [09:24]). The 9:17 statement may mean the opposite and is not treated as fact.
- **Out-of-date flags.** The item is TBU (4.0 [00:00]). The 5% charge and 48-hour rule are speech only in a TBU video (4.0 [05:02] [07:04] [09:38] [10:36]). The KPI name "non-time shipment ratio" is spoken, not shown (4.0 [11:41]), and is probably "on-time" (4.0 [11:41]). The Egypt description of Revibe teams may be out of date (4.0 [03:58]).
