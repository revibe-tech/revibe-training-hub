# 7. Refund policy

## What this topic covers

The returns refund policy in the Revibe App. It covers the four stages and their working-day (WD) deadlines, when the seller's clock runs and when it stops, which stages count against the seller in Saudi Arabia (SA) and in AE, ZA and other markets, how to raise a resolution request, the coloured markers on the claims list, and the Refund policy view on the Seller Performance page. The policy applies to returns only at the moment; warranties will follow once they are in the Revibe App (7.0 [00:24], [00:31]).

## How it works

1. Scope: the policy applies to returns only for now. Warranties follow once they are integrated into the Revibe App (7.0 [00:24], [00:31]).
2. Solve: the seller has 5 WD to solve a return, in all markets (7.0 [00:38], [00:42]).
3. Warning: if the return is still not solved, the seller gets a warning on the 6th WD in AE, ZA and other markets, or on the 7th WD in SA. The return turns yellow in the app (7.0 [00:43–00:59]).
4. Closing: if the return is still not solved, the closing stage follows on the 8th WD in AE, ZA and other markets, or on the 9th WD in SA. A full refund is issued to the customer and the claim is closed against the seller (7.0 [01:04–01:25]).
5. SA only, Collecting: before closing, Revibe collects the device. Once Revibe receives it, the customer is refunded and the seller is credited (7.0 [01:29–01:37]).
6. Clock definition: a WD is the sum of working-day hours during which the claim is pending only on the seller's side. Saturdays and Sundays never count, in any market (7.0 [01:41–02:04]).
7. Clock stops: the clock runs only while the claim sits on the seller's side and stops while it is on Revibe's side (7.0 [03:41], [04:48–04:56]).
8. Resolution requests: if there is an issue on the seller's side, for example a device issue, the seller creates a resolution request. The clock stops straight away and restarts only when the resolution is solved, not when a Revibe agent replies (7.0 [02:10–02:22], [06:37]).
9. Which stages count (SA): 9 stages run the seller's clock. Under collection is never counted (7.0 [03:03], [03:21–03:27], [05:40]).
10. Which stages count (AE, ZA and others): 3 stages run the seller's clock: Under QC, Under revision and Pending LAB collection. Collection and ship-back are run by Revibe (7.0 [03:03], [04:37–04:44]).
11. SA start day: the SA count starts on day 1, because the seller must create the AWB bill and nothing moves until it is issued (7.0 [03:07–03:18], [04:58–05:01]).
12. Raising a resolution request: open the claim, go to the claim detail page, open Revibe Resolutions and click Add Resolution. Blockers such as device issue, wrong item and missing information go in this way. Once created, no working hours are counted against the seller on that claim (7.0 [06:00–06:31]).
13. Spotting claims at risk: the coloured marker on each row of the claims list shows the status. Yellow is a warning, orange is policy to be applied (SA only), red is policy applied, and cyan is Resetted (7.0 [06:42–07:57]).
14. Monitoring: open Seller Performance > Claims management > Refund policy. Click a day column in a market chart to open a side drawer that lists that column's claims with claim ID, market, days on clock, timer and stage. Copy the claim ID to the orders app to solve the claim quickly (7.0 [08:41], [10:12–10:26]). The orders app is named by the trainer but not shown on screen.
15. Questions: send them to the seller's key account manager (7.0 [10:37]).

## Contradictions

- Policy day basis: the expanded Methodology text says 24 working hours and only the seller's hours count (7.0 [09:47], [09:25]). The chart note and the side-drawer footnote say 24 weekday hours on the Dubai calendar day, and that Saturdays and Sundays never accrue (7.0 [10:00], [10:24]). The video does not say which wording is correct.
- Units: Parts 1 and 2 measure warnings and closing in working days (WD). The dashboard measures them in policy days. The video does not explain how the two relate (7.0 [00:43], [09:01], [10:00]).
- SA warning: the policy card gives the warning on the 7th WD (7.0 [00:45]). The dashboard shows warning days 7 and 8 for SA (7.0 [08:59–09:15]). The two are not clearly reconciled.
- Yellow marker timing: the speech says the marker shows "after day 6", while the screen says "Day 6" for AE, ZA and others and "day 7" for SA. It is not confirmed whether the marker shows on or after day 6 (7.0 [06:48], [06:52]).
- Collection failed: the speech says the seller creates a "ROE bill", while the card and the rest of the video say the AWB is the bill the seller creates (7.0 [03:35], [03:11], [05:03]). The sentence is cut off.
- Ship-back destination: the speech says the device goes "to the customer"; the screen does not say whether it goes to the customer, the lab or Revibe (7.0 [04:34]).
- Under collection vs ship-back under collection: under collection is never counted (7.0 [05:48]), but the SA card counts "Ship back under collection" (7.0 [03:03]). The screen does not explain the difference.
- Under revision vs invalid claim: Under revision is listed as an SA seller-side stage (7.0 [03:03]). The item notes that Invalid claim sits on Revibe's side in the SA timeline (7.0 [04:05]). These are different stage names, so this is flagged rather than resolved.
- Orange and red markers both give day 9 in SA. The screen does not say how a claim moves from one to the other (7.0 [07:07], [07:28]).
- Dashboard snapshot: the slide shows 39 on the clock and the live page shows 37. These are different snapshots, not a conflict (7.0 [08:24], [09:22]). The "last meeting" text shows 94 days on the live page and 93 on the slide (7.0 [09:42]). This is not a policy rule.

## Quiz blueprint

Page 7.0 is 11:11 long, so the suggested count is 11 questions (one per minute, at most 20 per video).

- Scope, deadlines and stage ladder (about 2 questions): Solve 5 WD; warning 6/7 WD; closing 8/9 WD; returns only. Single-answer.
- Weekends and clock definition (1 question): Saturdays and Sundays do not count; WD definition. Single-answer.
- SA vs AE, ZA and others stage ownership (2 questions): suits multiple-answer. Ask the learner to select the seller-side stages in SA (9) and in AE, ZA and others (3), and which stages Revibe owns outside SA.
- Under collection and the SA collecting step (1 question): never counted; Revibe collects before closing. Single-answer.
- Resolution requests (2 questions): raise via claim page > Revibe Resolutions > Add Resolution; the clock freezes straight away and restarts only when solved, not on agent reply. Multiple-answer suits "which events stop the clock" (issue on the seller side, agent reply, resolution solved).
- Claims-list markers (2 questions): yellow, orange, red and cyan meanings; SA vs other-market day thresholds. Multiple-answer suits "which markers apply to SA" or "which markers mean the claim is closed against the seller".
- Dashboard navigation (1 question): Seller Performance > Claims management > Refund policy; side drawer from a day column. Single-answer.

Avoid questions on the unconfirmed items: the "9 WD +", the orders app, the 120/168 working-hour conversion, and the policy-day basis. Do not use the snapshot figures.

Scenario seeds (cited):

1. A return lands on a seller on a Monday. The seller raises a resolution request on Wednesday and it is solved on Friday. Explain that the clock froze on Wednesday, restarted on Friday, and that the seller still has 3 WD before a warning (7.0 [02:26–02:48]).
2. A seller has a wrong-item issue on a claim. Walk through the claim page, Revibe Resolutions and Add Resolution, and explain that the clock stops straight away (7.0 [06:07–06:23], [02:10]).
3. An SA seller's claim reaches the closing stage without being solved. Explain that Revibe collects the device first, then the customer is refunded in full and the claim closes against the seller (7.0 [01:18–01:37]).
