# 12. South Africa Bulk Shipments

## What this topic covers

How South Africa orders of the day ship in bulk. All of the day's South Africa orders travel together in one box, instead of one DHL label per order (12.0 [00:06]). Quiqup collects that box once a day (12.0 [00:14]). The topic covers the four daily steps, the seller-side screens under Bulk Shipping > Consolidation Panel, the packing and hand-over rules, and the two exceptions, Missing and Can't scan (12.0 [00:19], [01:16], [03:49], [04:01]).

Source pages: 12.0 only (Seller & KAM, 291 s).

## How it works

One consolidated walkthrough, in the order the page presents it.

1. Why: the old route gave every order its own DHL label, collected parcel by parcel. The new route packs all of the day's orders in one box. Quiqup collects it and scans each device at its warehouse, DHL flies the boxes of all suppliers to South Africa, and The Courier Guy delivers each device to the customer's door (12.0 [00:30], [00:36], [00:42], [00:48], [00:53]).
2. Step 1, Ready for pickup: in the seller panel's Orders page, click the purple Ready for pickup button on each order. No DHL label is produced. The order goes into today's open box, leaves the Orders table, and the Open box counter goes from 0 to 1 (12.0 [01:05], [01:10], [01:14]).
3. Find the box: Bulk Shipping > Consolidation Panel, Open box tab (12.0 [01:16], [01:20]).
4. Adjust while open: orders can be added and removed until the box is marked ready for collection. Remove takes an order out of the box and sends it back to Order created (12.0 [01:37], [01:40]). The page does not show how an order is added (see Contradictions and gaps below).
5. Step 2, a barcode on every device: pack each device for the customer exactly as today, nobody opens it on the way, then print its barcode. The Barcode button on a row prints one device's barcode. Print all barcodes prints the whole box (12.0 [01:47], [01:50], [01:55], [02:00], [02:03]). Stick the label flat on the largest face of the device box (12.0 [02:12]). Never cut it, never fold it over an edge, and never crumple it or cover it with another label (12.0 [02:16], [02:17]).
6. Step 3, one big box: pack one box with all of the day's devices, using the Quiqup box and the red tape that Quiqup sends (12.0 [02:24], [02:28], [02:31]). The box must match the open box list: every device on the list goes in, and nothing else (12.0 [02:32], [02:35]). Before sealing, scan every barcode yourself, which proves each one reads and keeps a record (12.0 [02:39], [02:43]). If a listed device is not in the box, press Remove on its row, otherwise it comes back as Missing (12.0 [02:46], [02:50]). Seal every seam with red tape, and film the packing and sealing on your phone. The video is your proof if a count is ever questioned (12.0 [02:52], [02:58]).
7. Step 4, hand-over: press Mark ready for collection only when the Quiqup rider is with you, usually around 3 PM. Do not press it early. Once pressed, the box is closed and devices can no longer be added or removed (12.0 [03:05], [03:11], [03:12], [03:16]). Then print the Quiqup label with the Print Quiqup label button under Pickup AWB label. It is one label for the whole box. Print it, stick it on top of the box, and cover it with clear tape (12.0 [03:17], [03:21], [03:24]). The rider scans the label and takes the box. That completes the hand-over for the day (12.0 [03:30], [03:33]).
8. After hand-over: follow the box in the tracker, in the same panel, using the tabs Open box, With Quiqup, Scanned, With DHL and With TCG (12.0 [03:35], [03:40], [03:44]). An order turns Shipped when Quiqup scans it (12.0 [00:45], [03:47]).
9. Exceptions: Missing means the device is on the list but was not in the box. The order goes back to Order created and the device goes in your next box (12.0 [03:49], [03:51], [03:55], [03:57]). Can't scan means the barcode was cut, folded or unreadable. Quiqup sends the device back the next day. Reprint its barcode and put it in a new box (12.0 [04:01], [04:06], [04:09]).
10. Daily checklist: print it and keep it next to the packing table. The six items are Ready for pickup on every order, a barcode on every device, the box matches the open box list, anything not in the box removed, sealed with red tape and filmed, and mark ready for collection with the label on top when the rider arrives (12.0 [04:18], [04:23], [04:25], [04:28], [04:30], [04:33], [04:39]). Questions go to your Revibe account manager (12.0 [04:43], [04:44]).

## Contradictions

Only items the page itself leaves unresolved or flags. None of these is a direct conflict between two statements, but they should not be taught as settled.

- Device count at hand-over: the packing list shows Scanned 0/6 and the Box #1 counters show 6 devices (12.0 [02:33], [01:20]). The hand-over panel's button reads Print all barcodes (6), but its table shows four device rows (12.0 [03:04]). The page does not explain how the two relate. Do not state how many devices a box holds at hand-over.
- Example device in the Missing panel: ZA-85654-1 is used as the example in the Missing panel. The page does not say it is an example, and the same device shows as Added and then Scanned elsewhere in the video (12.0 [03:53], [03:04], [04:10]). Do not present it as a real missing device.
- "Pickup failed" is said to mean one of two things, but the narration does not name them on that slide. Missing and Can't scan are the exceptions shown earlier (12.0 [04:16], [03:49], [04:01]). Treat the two causes as inferred.
- DHL label callout: a "DHL label" callout appears above the Orders table while the narration says sellers no longer get a DHL label (12.0 [01:10]). The page itself flags this line as describing the change away from per-order DHL labels.
- Possibly out of date: the narration at 00:30 describes the old per-order DHL process in the past tense. Use it only as background (12.0 [00:30]).
- Gaps, not conflicts: the add-order action is never shown (12.0 [01:40]). The tracker stages Packing, Collected, Shipped, Flying and Delivering appear on screen, but only Shipped is explained (12.0 [03:37]). When the Quiqup order number field fills in is not shown (12.0 [02:00]).
