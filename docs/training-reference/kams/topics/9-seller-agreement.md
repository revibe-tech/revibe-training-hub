# 9. Seller agreement

## What this topic covers

The Revibe Seller Agreement, walked through clause by clause for Sellers and Key Account Managers (KAMs). Covered: onboarding and the company details form, the commission model, customer contact rules, order shipping deadlines and penalties, weekly payments and the payment account rule, required documents, VAT scheme choice, product grades and battery thresholds, returns, warranty, claim timelines, shipping responsibility by country, packaging, data wiping and chargers, the seller score, and termination. Source: 9.0 [00:00] to [03:41].

## How it works

Order below follows the video.

1. **Onboarding.** Download the agreement, fill in company details offline (Company Name, Trade License No., exact and detailed principal place of business), then upload it on the onboarding form (9.0 [00:04], [00:05]).
2. **Commission model.** Revibe sells the Seller's products on commission. The Seller fulfils and ships Orders. The Seller provides availability and an up-to-date price list and lists prices only for items it can effectively supply (9.0 [00:11], [00:16]).
3. **Customer contact.** Revibe handles customer communication, complaints and after-sales, including receipt, aggregation and filtering of claims. The Seller never contacts a customer directly without Revibe's prior written authorization (9.0 [00:20], [00:24]).
4. **Order handling.** Ship every Order within 48 working hours of receiving it. Do not mark an Order shipped until the device has been dispatched. Revibe provides Order updates through a shared system and may cancel any Order before shipment (9.0 [00:29], [00:30], [00:35]).
5. **KSA courier status.** If the courier is integrated with Revibe by API, status syncs automatically and must not be updated manually. If not integrated, the Seller updates status manually, in line with the courier's status (9.0 [00:30]).
6. **Penalties.** Refused "not available" order: 5%. Customer cancels for a Seller-caused delay: 5%. Marked shipped before dispatch: 20%. Not shipped within 15 calendar days of confirmation: 10% of order value, and Revibe may cancel and reassign (9.0 [00:36], [00:37], [00:42]). Penalties are deducted from the balance or invoiced separately, at Revibe's discretion (9.0 [00:36]).
7. **Weekly statement and payment.** Revibe issues a statement every Monday and pays the Orders on it the same week, between Tuesday and Friday. COD Orders are invoiced on delivery instead (9.0 [00:48], [00:49]). If the net balance is below AED 5,000, payment may be withheld and carried forward to the next weekly cycle in which the threshold is met (9.0 [00:51]).
8. **Payment account.** Revibe pays only by bank transfer, and only to a company bank account in the Seller's official registered name. Personal accounts, accounts of another person or company, and mismatched names are never paid, even under an assignment or factoring agreement. The only exception is Revibe's written approval given in advance. The rule cannot be changed; it is required by AML, sanctions and beneficial ownership law (9.0 [00:56], [00:57], [01:02], [01:03]).
9. **Discontinuation.** Partnership is presumed discontinued if the Seller made no shipments in the last 5 working days, or if 30% or more of claims from the last 2 months are unresolved (and at least 10 uncovered claims). In that case Revibe may retain AED 10,000 from amounts otherwise due, for warranty and return cases (9.0 [00:57]).
10. **Required documents.** Keep valid: trade licence, corporate bank account confirmation letter, Tax Registration Certificate (TRC), and the Emirates ID of the authorized signatory and/or beneficial owner. Report any change, expiry or renewal promptly, send renewed licences within 10 calendar days of issue and no later than the old expiry date, and start renewal before expiry. If a document is missing, expired or invalid, Revibe may withhold payments, suspend the account and reject new Orders (9.0 [01:07], [01:08], [01:12]).
11. **VAT.** The Seller is the VAT supplier and Revibe issues Self-Billed Invoices in the Seller's name. At signing the Seller ticks one scheme: Option A (Standard), Option B (Profit Margin) or Option C (not VAT registered). Option A charges 5% VAT on the full selling price, shown separately on the Self-Billed Invoice. Option B needs clause 6.15.3 completed (9.0 [01:15], [01:18], [01:22]).
12. **Grades.** Phones are graded New, Excellent, Very Good and Good. New is sealed and never activated. Excellent is battery above 95%. Very Good is battery above 90% with light scratches only. Good is battery above 85% with visible scratches. Every grade must be 100% tested and fully functional. Deviation from the New criteria is misrepresentation (9.0 [01:28], [01:31], [01:36], [01:40], [01:43], [01:30]).
13. **Battery and screen after delivery.** Battery loss above 10% in 6 months, or above 20% in 12 months, means a free replacement including logistics. A message that the battery or another part is not original means a full refund. Normal battery wear is not a warranty defect. The screen is covered if there is no customer-caused breakage. Suspected breakage needs clear pictures or video (9.0 [01:46], [01:47], [01:53], [01:37]).
14. **Evidence.** For every dispatched Order, film the device at packing and dispatch. This proof is a precondition for any later claim about condition. For every returned device, record an unpacking video on receipt. Without this proof the Seller cannot dispute a claim (9.0 [01:57], [02:03]).
15. **Claims and returns.** A claim is valid if the Seller agrees, or disagrees but Revibe reviews and confirms it. A claim is invalid if the Seller says so and Revibe confirms after review. Revibe's decision is final. Customers may return within 15 days of delivery for any valid reason, including change of mind or product issues (9.0 [02:07], [02:10]).
16. **Return-to-origin credits.** UAE and South Africa change of mind or refusal: Revibe pays shipping, no deduction, Seller credited 100% of the SKU price. KSA change of mind or refusal: Seller pays shipping, no deduction, Seller credited 80% of the SKU price minus AED 150; Seller keeps the device plus 20% plus AED 150 (9.0 [02:16] to [02:23]).
17. **Warranty.** Every device has a 12-month warranty from delivery, covering repair, replacement or refund. Exclusions: misuse, physical or accidental damage including broken screens, water damage, normal battery wear, and LCD issues under clause 7.6 (9.0 [02:29], [02:31]). Valid claim: the product returns to the Seller for repair or replacement. In UAE and South Africa, Revibe arranges shipping and a fixed AED 30 is deducted from the Seller. In KSA, the Seller arranges logistics (9.0 [02:37], [02:38], [02:40]).
18. **Invalid claims.** UAE and South Africa, customer does not pay shipping: Revibe keeps the device, no credit. KSA, customer does not pay shipping: Revibe keeps the device and the Seller is compensated 20% of the SKU price plus AED 150. Where the customer pays shipping, the device goes back to the customer (9.0 [02:38]).
19. **Claim resolution.** Resolve within 5 working days of receiving the device. If still unresolved after 6 working days because of Seller non-cooperation, Revibe may refund the customer and recover it from the Seller. After 8 working days Revibe refunds the customer regardless and recovers it from the Seller. The Seller must receive returned devices and tell Revibe, in every country (9.0 [02:47], [02:48], [02:50]).
20. **Shipping by country.** Revibe ships UAE and South Africa Orders. For KSA, the Seller ships by air using a Revibe-approved partner (DHL, UPS, Aramex, SMSA, RSA or Delivery Panda) so the customer has a tracking ID, and covers all related charges including customs. If the customer paid delivery fees in KSA, Revibe reimburses them plus AED 50 and charges the total back to the Seller (9.0 [02:55], [02:56], [02:58]).
21. **Packaging.** Always use Revibe packaging. If it runs out, tell Revibe immediately and use alternative packing until restocked. Revibe's quality guidelines apply, and NSYS may be required for iPhone diagnostics (9.0 [03:04], [03:05], [03:06]).
22. **Data wipe and chargers.** Factory reset every device and remove personal data before shipping. The Seller bears full responsibility; Revibe is not liable for data left on devices. A faulty or missing charger can be claimed within 10 days of receiving the order. Revibe refunds the customer and debits the Seller (9.0 [03:12], [03:14]).
23. **Score.** The score runs from -10% to +10% and reflects operational performance and price positioning. Sellers see a Performance and Score sheet. A poor score can suspend the Seller's own shipping, leaving Revibe's warehouse as the only route to keep trading in the UAE and South Africa (9.0 [03:21], [03:25], [03:28]).
24. **Exit.** Either side can terminate on 30 days' written notice. After termination the Seller fulfils Orders confirmed before the notice date, resolves claims received before it, and handles claims arising within 12 months after the last Order delivery. Revibe settles amounts due within 180 days of the last Order delivery. The Seller keeps communication open and reports stock issues or delays immediately (9.0 [03:31], [03:32], [03:34], [03:39]).
25. **Questions.** Questions about the Seller Agreement go to the Seller's Key Account Manager (9.0 [03:41], [03:44]).

## Contradictions

- **48 hours.** The recap slide says "48h shipping" (9.0 [03:44]). Clause 3.4 says 48 working hours of receiving the Order (9.0 [00:29]). Use the clause. Do not tell Sellers 48 calendar hours.
- **Return reason wording.** The narration says returns are accepted "for any reason" (9.0 [02:07]). The screen says "for any valid reason, including change of mind or product issues" (9.0 [02:07]). Use the screen wording.
- **Withholding roll-over.** The narration says a sub-threshold balance rolls "to the next week" (9.0 [00:51]). The clause says it is settled in the next weekly cycle in which the threshold is met (9.0 [00:51]).
- **KSA courier status wording.** The clause 3.5 text reads "shall update" the status manually (9.0 [00:30]). A later KSA bullet from the 9.0 page reads "should update" (9.0 [02:48]). That bullet's clause number is not visible, so it is not relied on here.
- **Settlement base.** The narration says Revibe pays "within 180 days" after termination (9.0 [03:34]). The clause runs the 180 days from the last Order delivery (9.0 [03:34], [03:39]).
- **Scope of the 12-month window.** The narration says the Seller handles open orders and claims for 12 months (9.0 [03:34]). The clause limits the 12-month window to claims arising after the last Order delivery, not all open work (9.0 [03:34]).
- **Packing penalty.** The narration gives AED 200 without conditions (9.0 [03:06]). The clause applies it only where the Seller did not report a shortage or still held Revibe packing stock (9.0 [03:05], [03:06]).
- **Charger values.** The narration says "30 to 70 dirhams" and omits the laptop value (9.0 [03:14]). The on-screen list is AED 30 phone, AED 50 laptop and AED 70 MacBook (9.0 [03:12]).
- **Wording of the KSA credit example.** The quiz material says "Revibe recovers AED 650" for an AED 1,000 KSA refusal (9.0 [02:17]). The rule says the Seller is credited 80% minus AED 150, which is AED 650, and keeps the device plus AED 350 (9.0 [02:23]). Use the credit wording.
- **Unconfirmed items, not contradictions, but do not state as fact.**
  - The 7-day VAT scheme change rule is said by the trainer (9.0 [01:22]) but not shown on any frame.
  - The 3% in 10 days battery bullet is shown on screen, but its lead-in is cut off and the trainer does not mention it (9.0 [01:47]). Medium confidence.
  - Clause 9.2 (invalid claim re-evaluation) is cut off after step (a) (9.0 [02:48]).
  - The Option B eligibility list in clause 6.15.3 is cut off (9.0 [01:18]).
  - A rule that the Seller should not issue its own separate invoice for a sale Revibe already invoices is implied only (9.0 [01:15], [01:21]).
- **Out of date.** None found.
