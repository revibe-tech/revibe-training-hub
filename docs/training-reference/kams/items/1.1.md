### Rules and policies

- ★ Revibe currently operates in three countries: UAE and South Africa, where Revibe manages shipment for the seller, and Saudi Arabia (KSA), where the seller manages shipment [00:25] [00:32] [00:22 t00022].
- ★ Payment: Revibe pays the seller the price the seller asks, with no hidden commission, and pays every week [00:49] [00:53] [00:42 t00042].
- ★ Warranty: the seller is asked to offer customers 6 months of warranty for valid claims [01:22 t00082]. The speech says the seller will be asked to "support the first six months of warranty" [01:22].
- ★ The app is for sellers who want to join Revibe as official partners: shops, wholesalers and online sellers who want an ongoing relationship [04:27 t00270] [04:34].
