## Summary

This training explains the South Africa bulk shipment process. All of a seller's South Africa orders for the day go into one box that Quiqup collects once a day, instead of one DHL label per order [00:06] [00:09 t00009] [00:14 t00015]. It is for sellers and KAMs. The day has four steps: Ready for pickup in the seller panel, a barcode on every device, one big box, and the hand-over to Quiqup [00:19] [00:28 t00029]. Steps 1, 2 and 4 are done in the panel; step 3 is physical [00:28 t00029]. The video covers all four steps in full. The packing and hand-over detail is in the second half of the video (about 02:20 to 03:37).
