## Summary

This 20-minute video walks KAMs through the Buybox tab of a seller's Google Sheet and the Buybox AI workbook. It explains how the buy box picks a winning seller using seller score and price, how to read the Buybox, Wins and Price columns, and how the red (AE) and green (SA) shipping rules differ. It also covers the minimum price function, the AE and SA price-drop tabs, the payment tabs, and the lowest-conditions SKUs. It is intended for KAMs.

## What a KAM needs to know

### Rules and policies

- ★ The Buy Box is one unique catalogue with several sellers bidding on the same SKUs; for one SKU, several sellers can try to win it. [00:11] [00:21]
- ★ The Buy Box is the algorithm that selects the best seller able to sell a SKU. [00:29] [00:37]
- ★ If another seller drops price, or a seller's score rises, that seller may also be in a position to win the SKU ("maybe"). [00:50] [00:58]
- ★ For direct shipping in all countries, sellers put in prices only, not quantities. [01:02] [01:11] [01:14]
- ★ The catalogue is set by Revibe. Sellers cannot sort the rows or change their order; the only catalogue action they have is filtering. [01:25] [01:42]
- ★ A seller with a better score can compete at a higher price than a seller with a neutral score. [05:51] [06:01]
- ★ To win a SKU, the seller has to price one dirham lower than the current price. [06:29] [06:36]
- ★ AE is the United Arab Emirates and SA is Saudi Arabia. The AE columns are red/pink and the SA price columns are green. [03:31] [03:56] [t00198]
- ★ Red countries are the UAE, South Africa, and all new countries where Revibe provides a shipping solution. [03:41] [03:47]
- ★ In red countries, sellers do not pay shipping fees. Revibe pays them and provides the airway bills, which the seller puts on the box. [04:11] [04:20] [04:28]
- ★ In the green country (Saudi Arabia, the only one at the time of the video), the seller must have his own contract with a courier company, pay the fees, ship DDP, and create the airway bill himself. [04:04] [04:35] [04:43] [04:50]
- ★ Price drops stop once the Wins column shows TRUE (the narration says "until here it's showing true" and does not name the column), because Revibe does not want sellers to end up with too low prices. [13:10] [13:15] [13:17]
- ★ For lowest-conditions SKUs, sellers compete with each other only on price, not on score. [19:11] [19:19]
- ★ Revibe wants to appear as the marketplace with the lowest price on these very specific devices. [20:37]
- Sellers can filter the Buybox tab by model, storage, condition, SKU and other columns. [02:01] [t00111]
- The AE tool and SA tool each have purple SMALL PRICE DROP and BIG PRICE DROP buttons above their tables. [14:28] [t00868] [t01050]
- Only SKUs where the seller has put a price and is not winning appear in the AE and SA tools. [17:38] [t01074]
- Dated weekly payment tabs show each week's payments; sellers can see how much they will be paid. [18:09] [18:15] (outdated: see Problems)

### Procedures

- ★ Read Price AE against the neutral price and the seller's score: Price AE = Neutral Buybox AE x (1 + score). [05:41] [05:51] [t00351]
- ★ To accept price changes, the seller ticks Accept? for each SKU he agrees to, then presses the purple button. The press runs a script. [15:23] [15:32] [t00923] [t00940]
- ★ If the script asks for authorization, click OK, continue in the Google window, select all permissions, and continue. [15:44] [15:53] [16:02] [t00962]
- ★ After a seller enters a new price, the algorithm synchronises and the Wins column shows TRUE (the timing is unclear). [06:43] [06:47] [06:55] (medium)
- ★ To see the score in the seller portal, open the Reporting bookmark and the Revibe Workspace, then the Seller Performance page and choose the seller. The links between the pages are not narrated. [04:54] [05:22] [t00316] [t00321] [t00325]
- ★ Implied: to check which seller wins a SKU, open that seller's sheet, search for the SKU in column H (SKU_Long), and read Buybox AE and Wins AE on that row. The trainer does this for SF only. [09:11] [09:15] [t00551]

- ★ Hide columns Y to AG (select, right-click, Hide columns) so the seller cannot open the formula and pricing columns. [13:50] [13:55] [t00828]
- ★ To see who changed a price cell, right-click the cell and choose Show edit history. The popover shows an editor name, a date and time, and a Replaced entry with the old and new values. [16:36] [16:43] [16:48] [t01008]
