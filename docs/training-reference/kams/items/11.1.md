## Summary

This video shows sellers how to sign in to the Seller Performance dashboard and how to read it. Part 1 covers the header, then the Performance tab: timeframes, country and category filters, the top KPI tiles, and the Orders, GMV, On time, Defect rate and Days to solve sections. Part 2 covers Claims coverage, the Scoring tab's next score table, and the Analysis tab that explains each KPI. The audience is sellers; this page is for team leads and new KAMs who need to explain the dashboard to them. Every dashboard screen shows a demo seller (Seller ABC) on a localhost demo build, but the sign-in screens do not show a seller, so all figures are sample data and must not be read as a current score [00:48 t00048] [05:01 t00301].

## What a KAM needs to know

Items marked ★ are quiz-worthy. Items marked "(unconfirmed)" are low confidence. Items marked "(medium confidence)" are medium confidence and should be checked before they are repeated.

### Rules and policies

- ★ The email and password option for sellers and repair partners uses the same email address and password as admin.revibe.me. There is no separate password for this dashboard [00:25 t00025] [00:27].
- ★ The next score is built from four KPIs: On time (weight 10%), Defect rate (60%), Days to solve (20%) and Claims coverage (10%) [05:26 t00326] [05:29].
- ★ Claims coverage is counted against the month the claim was received (monthly view), or the week it was received (weekly view) [05:02 t00303] [05:14 t00314].
- ★ The claims coverage divisor is received minus cancelled. A withdrawn claim never had to be solved, so it counts neither for nor against the seller [05:02 t00303].
- ★ A claim counts as solved when a warranty's device ships back, or a return's money reaches the customer. These are the same endings Days to solve uses [05:02 t00303].
- ★ Claims coverage is keyed on the repair partner, meaning the claims you repaired. Change-of-mind claims and claims ruled invalid are excluded [05:02 t00303].
- ★ Days to solve is keyed on the repair partner. It covers the claims you repaired, never claims on your orders that another partner handled [05:06 t00277].
- ★ Days to solve counts only claims that reached a resolved ending and carry the timestamp needed to measure it. A claim still in progress is left out, not scored as zero days [05:06 t00277].
- ★ Defect rate uses qualifying warranty and return claims, set against confirmed orders [03:03 t00163].
- ★ Excluded from the defect rate: change-of-mind, cancelled-form, installment-failure and RTO claims [03:03 t00163].
- ★ Both claims and orders are counted against the order's month, not the claim's month. The rate reads as "of what I sold in March, how much came back" [03:03 t00163] [07:01 t00421].
- ★ The 30-day and 90-day defect rates become final once that many days have passed since the month closed. The unbounded (full) rate never settles, so a recent month always reads low [03:03 t00163].
- ★ The 30-day and 90-day defect rates are the same rate over the same orders. They count only claims filed within 30 or 90 days of the order [03:03 t00163] [03:52 t00212].
- The 30-day rate is never above the 90-day rate, and the 90-day rate is never above the full rate [03:03 t00163].
- The three on-time readings (On time, On time shipment, On time delivery) come from the same rows and the same confirmed-date clock. Switching between them changes the definition, never the population [02:36 t00138].
- ★ Shipment and delivery on-time rates are pooled: on-time orders of both are divided by orders of both. They are not averaged as two separate rates [02:36 t00138].
- ★ A month whose SLA clock has not run out yet is not judged [02:36 t00138].
- ★ A market with fewer than 10 judged orders in a month does not get its own on-time figure. It still counts inside all markets, weighted by its own size [02:36 t00138].
- ★ Outside Saudi, an order is late (missed) if it shipped 3 or more working days after confirmation (medium confidence) [06:21 t00381].
- ★ In Saudi Arabia, an order is late (missed) if it arrived outside the 5-working-day promise (medium confidence) [06:21 t00381] [07:04].
- ★ The SLA column sets the threshold for each row. SA rows show "SLA <= 5". The ZA and AE shipment rows show "SLA < 3" [07:47 t00431].
- ★ All day columns on the on-time analysis are working days, weekends excluded. The red figure is the one that made the order a miss [06:21 t00381].
- ★ The On time analysis reads orders over the window 17-23 Aug. Every order that missed in that window is listed [06:21 t00381].
- ★ For a return, days to solve ends when the refund reaches the customer. For a warranty, it ends when the device ships back. Where a warranty ended in a refund instead, the day that refund landed is used [08:13] [08:17] [08:44 t00488].
- ★ A return is resolved when the refund reaches the customer. A warranty is resolved when the device ships back to them, or when it is refunded instead [05:06 t00277] [04:44].
- All claims days to solve is total days over total claims resolved. It is not the average of the Returns and Warranties averages, so it is weighted by whichever kind you handled [05:06 t00277].
- Defect rate counts one row per order. The Analysis tab says two claims on the same order count as one defect [08:05 t00449]. The Part 1 Defect rate methodology says two claims on the same item count once [03:03 t00163]. The two wordings conflict, so this rule is not settled (see Problems found).
- ★ The claims coverage analysis lists the claims from 22 Jun to 23 Aug that are still not solved, oldest first, with their days open. Each one counts against the seller until it is solved. Cancelled claims are not listed [09:02 t00506].

### Definitions

- ★ Buy box status is shown in the dashboard header as open or closed (the screen shows "Buybox open") [00:38] [00:48 t00048].
- ★ Orders counts confirmed orders, counted on the day each order was confirmed [02:13 t00116].
- ★ One order is one confirmed order line, so a three-item order counts as three [02:13 t00116].
- Orders are split by the market the order shipped to. Other is every market under 1.5% of the window, added together [02:13 t00116].
- ★ The top KPI tiles show the selected period and compare each figure with the previous period. For a month the comparison is vs. Jun 26; for a week, vs. Aug 17; for a day, vs. Sep 2 [01:17] [01:23] [01:51 t00097] [00:48 t00048] [01:27 t00077].
- ★ On time is a composite score: each market is judged on the leg Revibe actually holds the seller to [02:36 t00138] [02:30].
- ★ Within 30 days of the order and within 90 days of the order are the same rate over the same orders, counting only claims filed within that many days of the order [03:03 t00163] [03:52 t00212].
- ★ Days to solve is calendar days from the claim date to the claim being resolved, using the same clock in every market [05:06 t00277] [04:39].
- ★ Claims coverage is, of the claims you were given to solve, the share you have actually solved, judged as of today [05:02 t00303].
