## What a KAM needs to know

### Rules and policies

- ★ The Buybox sets prices for direct shipping orders to customers in Saudi Arabia, UAE and South Africa. [00:12] [00:17] [00:00 t00000]
- ★ For UAE and South Africa, Revibe handles the shipment and pays the shipping cost. The trainer says orders in UAE are delivered by partner QuickUp and South Africa orders by partner DHL (names from speech only; not on screen). [02:09] [02:16] [02:18] [02:25] [02:27]
- ★ For Saudi Arabia, the seller pays their own shipping cost and has to get a partnership with one delivery company. [02:31] [02:33]
- ★ The seller competes on the buy box price, which is the neutral price adjusted by the seller's score. [03:52] [03:58]
- ★ For lowest-condition devices (lowest storage, most basic colour, lowest quality condition), the score has no impact and sellers compete only on price. [04:31] [04:37] [04:40] [04:47] [05:03] [14:14]
- ★ Small and big price drops can both be accepted, for UAE and for Saudi Arabia. [13:47] [13:52]
- The trainer says a seller who wants to win must put a price 1 dirham cheaper than the buy box price. Currency and unit are not confirmed on screen. (unconfirmed) [06:25]
- A seller's prices can be locked in UAE and South Africa by sending devices to the Revibe warehouse. Only announced in the agenda, not covered in this part. [00:59] [01:04]
