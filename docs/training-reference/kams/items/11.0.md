## Summary

This video is a guided tour of the Supply dashboard for KAMs (key account managers). It covers the Daily tab as a morning checklist, the KAM tab for the KAM's own book, performance and bonus, the Sellers tab for one seller at a time, the Scores tab with the weekly Thursday routine, and the Tools forms. It also gives quick looks at Team and Price recs, and at the KPIs and Periskope tabs, which are empty placeholders for now. It is for new KAMs learning the daily routine, and for team leads who need to check what each list means and what action it calls for. Several figures are snapshots dated 2026-10-16, and some screens (Cancellation, Sellers connections, the seller view) are only narrated or only partly shown.
