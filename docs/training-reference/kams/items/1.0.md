### Procedures

- ★ The seller onboarding process has four parts: 1. Inbound (sellers who come to Revibe on their own), 2. Outbound (sellers the KAM reaches out to recruit), 3. Seller view (what sellers see and must do in the onboarding app), 4. Admin view (how to process seller applications) [00:19 p6; 00:41; 07:52; 09:30].
