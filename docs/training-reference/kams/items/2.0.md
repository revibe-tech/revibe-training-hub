## What a KAM needs to know

### Rules and policies

- ★ Quantities allowed are deliberately limited so the seller does not send too much stock, so that Revibe can sell the stock within 15 days and pay the seller quickly, so the seller has money to buy new stock. [02:21] [02:28] [02:32] [02:37]
- ★ The SKUs available list shows only SKUs whose quantities allowed is greater than zero. [02:04]
- ★ The price shown on the SKUs available list is the same as the buy box price. [02:42] [02:48]
- The buy box price also depends on the seller score, which is explained in another video and not in this part. [02:48] [02:55]
- To send items to the warehouse, the seller has to beat the buy box by at least one DRAM (unit unconfirmed; not shown on screen). [03:00] [03:04]
- ★ The trainer says the Quantity Sent on a request line must be between one and the Quantity Allowed. [04:54] [05:07]
- ★ A Price above the Buybox supplier price is refused with the message "Come on! Let's beat Buybox!". [05:22] [05:26 t00326]
- ★ Payment method is a required field on the Requests Supplier Form. [04:13 t00253]
- ★ The payment method in use is 100% - After sales. [04:23] [04:28 t00265]
- ★ Request products that are Requested are either in a shipment at the requested stage, or in a shipment whose airway bill is created or that is picked up; in both cases the quality check has not been done yet. [06:44] [06:50] (medium confidence)
- ★ Complete QC must be done for the shipment, to avoid customer returns, warranty claims and rejection by Revibe. [08:15] [08:21]
- ★ Do not use Revibe boxes; pack everything properly in a carton box. [08:21] [08:30] (medium confidence)

- ★ Buybox supplier is the buy box reference price shown on each request line (608 for SKU 010109-060101, 370 for SKU 020134-038651, 639 for SKU 010116-060101). The exact meaning of the buy box is not explained on screen. (medium confidence) [05:17] [04:50 t00290] [06:10 t00376]

- ★ Total price is Price multiplied by Quantity Sent on the line; the trainer calls it the total value the seller is paid for. [07:50] [05:15 t00315]
- ★ Request Status values for a shipment are Requested, Picked up, Warehouse received and Cancelled. [03:43 t00223] [06:55 t00415]
