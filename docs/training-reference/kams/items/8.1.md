## Summary

This page set explains how a Revibe warranty claim moves from the customer's request to a final outcome. It covers who repairs the device (the original seller within one year, or the Revibe Repair Partner after one year or when misuse is declared), the seller's Valid or Invalid decision, the Lab step for AE, and the paid repair route under Revibe Care+ (RC+). It is written for sellers and KAMs who handle warranty claims. The source has no narration, so every fact comes from the PDF pages. Items marked (unconfirmed) need a KAM to check before relying on them.

## What a KAM needs to know

### Rules and policies

- ★ Within 1 year and with no misuse declared, the original seller is the repair supplier. [p1]
- ★ After 1 year, or when the customer declares misuse, the Revibe Repair Partner (Revibe Care+) repairs the device, and the original seller does not see the claim. [p1][p3]
- ★ For AE and ZA the AWB is created automatically and the claim moves to Under Collection immediately. For SA the seller creates the AWB manually. [p1]
- ★ A new AWB after a failed collection is created automatically for AE and ZA, and manually by the seller for SA. [p1]
- ★ If collection fails (the customer does not hand over the device), the agent asks whether the customer wants a new AWB. Yes returns the claim to Under collection; No cancels it. [p1]
- ★ Within 1 year, the seller marks the claim Valid (repair it) or Invalid (with reason and proof). Revibe reviews every Invalid claim. [p2]
- ★ An Invalid claim requires the seller to submit the reason and proof of invalidity. [p2]
- ★ After a seller Invalid, Revibe's agent either agrees (the device is sent to the Lab) or challenges (the agent marks the claim Valid with a rationale). [p2]
- ★ When the agent challenges and the seller must cover, the claim goes to Under revision; the seller marks it Valid or Invalid again, and the agent reviews again (Invalid goes to Lab). (unconfirmed: what "seller must cover" means and what cost is covered is not stated) [p2]
- ★ If the agent challenges an Invalid claim, misuse is found, and the customer has RC+, the seller gives a quotation (see the page 3 Route A). [p2][p3]
- ★ Lab collection happens for AE only. [p2]
- ★ Lab Valid: the customer is refunded and the device goes back to the seller. [p2]
- ★ Lab Invalid: the invalid claim is confirmed and the claim is closed. (unconfirmed: the same branch continues to ship-back, Delivered, Delivery failed and Device disposed; whether the claim closes before or after those steps is not clear) [p2]
- ★ After Lab Invalid, AE: Revibe ships the device back. ZA and SA: the customer pays the shipping fees, entered as a Stripe code once paid, with 90 days to pay. [p2]
- ★ Outside AE there is no Lab: the agent's confirmation of the seller's Invalid is final. [p2]
- ★ Route A (within 1 year, seller finds misuse): the agent marks "Misuse but customer has RC+", and the original seller gives a quotation and repairs the device, with Revibe covering the cost. Route A states no cap (unconfirmed: whether the 1,500 AED cap applies to Route A repairs). [p3]
- ★ For a quoted cost up to 1,500 AED, Revibe covers the repair. The extended warranty is consumed after the repair. [p3]
- ★ For a quoted cost above 1,500 AED: if the customer pays, the repair goes ahead; if not, there is no repair, the device is shipped back to the customer and RC+ is cancelled. [p3]
- ★ For a repair above 1,500 AED, the customer pays the difference (cost minus 1,500 AED). [p3]
- ★ If the customer refuses delivery of the ship-back, the claim shows Delivery failed; the agent asks whether the customer wants a new attempt. If yes, the seller pays a new AWB; if no, the device is disposed. (unconfirmed for AE and ZA: the page says the seller pays a new AWB, but for those markets the ship-back AWB is created by Revibe) [p2]
- If delivery fails on the ship-back of an RC+ repair, the agent offers a new attempt; if the customer refuses, the device is disposed. [p3]
- ★ Final outputs: Seller Valid means the device is repaired and shipped back; Lab Valid means the customer is refunded and the device goes back to the seller; Lab Invalid means the invalid claim is confirmed, with ship-back at the customer's cost (ZA and SA). [p2]
- (unconfirmed) Misuse routing: page 1 sends claims to the Revibe Repair Partner when misuse is declared by the customer, but page 3 Route A keeps seller-found misuse (within 1 year) with the original seller under RC+. The difference between customer-declared and seller-found misuse should be confirmed before relying on it. [p1][p3]

### Procedures

- The customer raises a warranty claim through the "My Account" section on Revibe; the claim status is Pending (Revibe team to review). [p1]
- At the Revibe agent check, missing documents are requested from the customer; if the customer does not provide them, the claim is Cancelled. [p1]
- Once the agent check is complete, the repair supplier is assigned and the status becomes Pending Collection. [p1]
- After the courier delivers the device, the seller sees the claim with status Under QC and must mark it Valid or Invalid. [p1]
- Valid path: after repair, the seller clicks "Repair finished"; the claim then moves to Ship back under collection, Shipped back and Delivered. [p2]
- Lab collection (AE only): a Quiqup AWB is created automatically, the seller hands the device to Quiqup, and the claim goes In transit to lab and then Lab under QC. [p2]
- Lab Valid flow: Pending lab to seller collection, In transit lab to seller, Delivered to seller. [p2]
- After Lab Invalid, the device follows the ship-back stages Ship back under collection, Shipped back and Delivered; if delivery fails, the device is either re-attempted (new attempt) or Device disposed. [p2]
- Quotation flow: the repairer enters the price, the Revibe agent reviews it; No sends it back to quotation; Yes sends it to a cost check. [p3]
- Route B: the device is sent straight to the Revibe Repair Partner; the partner inspects the device, identifies the issue and gives a quotation. [p3]
- Paid repair stages (up to 1,500 AED, or after payment above 1,500 AED): Ready for repair, Under repair, Repair finished, To ship back, Ship back under collection, Shipped back, Delivered. [p3]

### Numbers and deadlines

- ★ Within 1 year: the original seller repairs when no misuse is declared. The page does not say when the year starts (unconfirmed). [p1][p2]
- ★ 1,500 AED: the RC+ limit. Revibe covers repairs up to this amount; above it the customer pays the difference (cost minus 1,500 AED). [p3]
- 30 AED is deducted from the seller for an AE or ZA ship-back AWB created by Revibe. [p2]
- ZA and SA customers have 90 days to pay the ship-back shipping fees after Lab Invalid. [p2]

### Definitions

- ★ Valid: the seller decision that the claim is repaired by the original seller; the seller repairs and ships the device back. [p2]
- ★ Invalid: the seller decision that the claim is not valid; it requires reason and proof, and Revibe reviews it. [p2]
- Under QC: the claim status once the courier delivers the device to the seller; the seller then marks it Valid or Invalid. [p1]
- ★ Revibe Care+ (RC+) covers repairs up to 1,500 AED, and Revibe pays the repair. The page 3 title for this workflow is Paid Repairs. [p3]
- Revibe Repair Partner: the repairer used after 1 year or when the customer declares misuse; the original seller does not see the claim. It receives the device directly, inspects it, identifies the issue and gives a quotation. [p1][p3]
- AWB: the shipment document named on the page for pickup, new attempts and ship-back. The page says who creates it per market and does not expand the acronym. [p1]
- Lab: the lab verification step after an Invalid claim is reviewed by Revibe; available for AE only. [p2]
- Quiqup: named on the page as the party the seller hands the device to for lab collection in AE; a Quiqup AWB is auto-created. [p2]
- Misuse but customer has RC+: the agent status for a seller-found misuse claim where the customer holds RC+; the seller then gives a quotation. [p3]
- Seller quotation: the price entered by the repairer, reviewed by the Revibe agent before the cost check. [p3]
- Stripe code: the code entered once the customer has paid the ship-back shipping fee (ZA and SA after Lab Invalid). [p2]
- AE, ZA, SA: market codes used throughout the workflow to set different rules; the page does not spell out what each code stands for (unconfirmed). [p1]

### Warnings and tips

- ★ All refunds, credits and charges appear in the payment tool. [p1]
- (unconfirmed) The Lab Invalid branch is labelled "claim closed", yet it continues to ship-back and disposal steps. Confirm the order before relying on it. [p2]
- (unconfirmed) Who enters the Stripe code (seller, agent or customer) is not stated. [p2]

### Screens and fields

- The portal displays these tracking items: Tracking (original shipment), Pickup Tracking, Ship-Back and Lab. [p1]
- The seller sees two options on an Under QC claim: Mark VALID and Mark INVALID. [p1]
- Card action labels on the overview: Customer raise claim and Revibe Agent Check are NO ACTION REQUIRED; Pickup is ACTION REQUIRED IF SA; Seller action is ACTION REQUIRED. [p1]
- "Repair finished" is the button the seller clicks after Under repair on the Valid path. [p2]
- An Invalid claim needs a reason and proof of invalidity before it becomes Invalid claim. [p2]
