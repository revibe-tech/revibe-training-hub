## Summary

This 3-page workflow PDF, for sellers and KAMs, shows how Revibe handles a warranty claim from the moment the customer raises it to the final outcome [p1] [p2] [p3]. Page 1 sets who repairs: the original seller within 1 year with no misuse, or the Revibe Repair Partner (Revibe Care+) after 1 year or when the customer declares misuse, in which case the original seller doesn't see the claim [p1]. It then walks through the 4 steps (the customer raises the claim in My Account, a Revibe agent checks it, the device is picked up, and the seller acts at Under QC), with pickup AWBs auto-created in AE & ZA and created manually by the seller in SA [p1]. Page 2 covers the seller's decision: a Valid claim is repaired and shipped back, with 30 AED deducted from the seller in AE & ZA, while every Invalid claim needs a reason and proof and is reviewed by Revibe, with a Lab check in AE only [p2]. Page 3 covers Revibe Care+ paid repairs: RC+ covers repairs up to 1,500 AED, and above that the customer pays the difference, or else there is no repair, the device is shipped back and RC+ is cancelled [p3]. There is no narration, so everything here comes from the page text.

## What a KAM needs to know

★ = quiz-worthy. "(unconfirmed)" = the checkers had only medium confidence in this fact.

### Rules and policies

- ★ Who repairs a warranty claim: within 1 year and with no misuse, the original seller repairs the device. [p1]
- ★ If the claim comes after 1 year, or the customer declares misuse, the Revibe Repair Partner (Revibe Care+ / RC+) handles it, not the original seller. [p1]
- ★ Step 1 (Customer raise claim) and step 2 (Revibe Agent Check) are tagged NO ACTION REQUIRED. [p1]
- ★ Pickup in AE & ZA: the AWB is auto-created and the claim moves to Under Collection immediately. The Pickup step is tagged ACTION REQUIRED IF SA, so no seller action is flagged for AE & ZA at this step. [p1]
- ★ Pickup in SA: the seller must create the pickup AWB manually. The Pickup step is tagged ACTION REQUIRED IF SA. [p1]
- ★ After a failed collection, if the customer wants a new AWB: it is created automatically in AE & ZA and manually by the seller in SA, and the claim goes back to Under collection. [p1]
- ★ After a failed collection, if the customer does not want a new AWB, the claim is Cancelled. [p1]
- ★ Within 1 year, the seller inspects the device at Under QC and marks the claim Valid (repair it) or Invalid (reason + proof). [p2]
- ★ Revibe reviews every claim a seller marks Invalid. [p2]
- ★ Ship-back cost on a Valid claim in SA: the seller creates and pays the AWB. [p2]
- ★ Valid claim with failed delivery: if the customer wants a new attempt, the seller pays a new AWB and the claim goes back to Ship back under collection. [p2]
- ★ Valid claim with failed delivery: if the customer does not want a new attempt, the status becomes Device disposed. [p2]
- ★ To mark a claim Invalid, the seller must submit a reason and proof of invalidity. The claim then shows status Invalid claim. [p2]
- ★ When the agent's rationale is "Misuse but customer has RC+", the claim moves to a seller quotation (page 3, the Revibe Care+ paid-repair flow). [p2] [p3]
- ★ Lab collection (the Lab verification of Invalid claims) only exists in AE. [p2]
- ★ Lab Valid: the customer is refunded and the device goes back to the seller. Statuses: Pending lab to seller collection → In transit lab to seller → Delivered to seller. [p2]
- ★ Lab Invalid: the status becomes Invalid claim confirmed and the claim is closed. [p2]
- ★ After Lab Invalid in AE, Revibe ships the device back. [p2]
- ★ Outside AE there is no Lab yet, so the agent's confirmation of the seller's Invalid is final. [p2]
- ★ Under Revibe Care+, the repairer fixes the device and Revibe pays the repair. [p3]
- ★ After an RC+ repair covered by Revibe (≤ 1,500 AED), the extended warranty is consumed. [p3]
- ★ If the customer pays the difference above 1,500 AED, the repair goes ahead with the same stages as a repair at ≤ 1,500 AED. [p3]
- ★ If the customer does not pay the difference above 1,500 AED, there is no repair, the device is shipped back to the customer and RC+ is cancelled. [p3]
- RC+ ship-back delivery failed: the agent offers a new attempt; if it is refused, the device is disposed. [p3]

### Procedures

- ★ The customer raises a warranty claim through the "My Account" section on Revibe. The claim starts in status Pending, for the Revibe team to review. [p1]
- ★ In the Revibe Agent Check, the agent checks the claim info. Missing documents are requested from the customer, and if the customer does not provide them, the claim is Cancelled. [p1]
- ★ Once the agent check is complete, the repair supplier is assigned and the claim status becomes Pending Collection. [p1]
- ★ Once the Courier Company delivers, the seller can see the claim and its status becomes Under QC. This step (Seller action) is tagged ACTION REQUIRED. [p1]
- ★ Claim status order up to the seller decision: Pending → Pending Collection → Under Collection → In Transit → Under QC. [p1]
- ★ Collection failed means the customer doesn't hand over the device. The agent then asks the customer if they want a new AWB. [p1]
- ★ Valid claim status flow: Under repair → the seller clicks "Repair finished" → Ship back under collection → Shipped back → Delivered. [p2]
- ★ If the customer refuses the ship-back delivery of a repaired device, the status becomes Delivery failed and the agent asks the customer if they want a new attempt. [p2]
- The Invalid path runs Proof Required → Revibe Review → Lab Verification. [p2]
- ★ If the Revibe agent agrees with the seller's Invalid, the device is sent to the Lab. Lab collection is AE only; outside AE there is no Lab yet and the agent's confirmation is final. [p2]
- ★ If the Revibe agent challenges the seller's Invalid, the agent marks the claim Valid with a rationale. [p2]
- ★ When the agent's rationale is "Seller must cover", the claim goes to Under revision. The seller marks it Valid or Invalid again and the agent reviews again; if it is Invalid, it goes to the Lab. [p2]
- ★ Lab collection in AE: the status is Pending lab collection, a Quiqup AWB is auto-created and the seller hands the device to Quiqup. Then In transit to lab → Lab under QC. [p2]
- Ship-back after a confirmed Invalid claim: Ship back under collection → Shipped back → Delivered. If delivery fails, there is a new attempt, or the device is disposed (Device disposed). [p2]
- ★ Route A (within 1 year, original seller finds misuse): the seller marks Invalid (misuse). The agent sees that the customer has RC+ and marks "Misuse but customer has RC+". The original seller then gives a quotation and repairs the device, and Revibe covers the cost. [p3]
- ★ Route B (after 1 year, or misuse declared): the device is sent straight to the Revibe Repair Partner, who inspects it, identifies the issue and gives a quotation. [p3]
- ★ RC+ quotation flow: status Seller quotation (the repairer enters the price) → Seller quotation review → the Revibe agent approves or not. Not approved → back to quotation. Approved → cost check. [p3]
- ★ RC+ repair at ≤ 1,500 AED: Revibe covers the repair. Statuses: Ready for repair → Under repair → Repair finished → To ship back → Ship back under collection → Shipped back → Delivered. [p3]
- ★ RC+ repair above 1,500 AED: the status is Pending customer payment and the customer pays (cost − 1,500 AED). [p3]

### Numbers and deadlines

- ★ Ship-back cost on a Valid claim in AE & ZA: the AWB is made by Revibe and 30 AED is deducted from the seller. [p2]
- (unconfirmed) After Lab Invalid: in AE, Revibe ships the device back; in ZA & SA, the customer pays the shipping fees (Stripe code entered once paid) and has 90 days to pay. Not starred and kept out of the quiz until it is clarified, because Lab is AE only (see Unclear passages, p2). [p2]
- ★ Revibe Care+ (RC+) covers repairs up to 1,500 AED. [p3]
- ★ Under Revibe Care+, if the repair costs more than 1,500 AED, the customer pays the difference. [p3]

### Definitions

- ★ Status In Transit: the device has been picked up from the customer. [p1]
- Final output of a Valid claim: the seller fixes the device and ships it back to the customer. [p2]
- Final outputs of the seller-decision flow: Seller Valid → device repaired and shipped back. Lab Valid → customer refunded, device back to seller. Lab Invalid → invalid claim confirmed, ship-back at customer cost (ZA & SA). [p2]

### Warnings and tips

- ★ On Route B, the Revibe Repair Partner route (after 1 year, or misuse declared by the customer), the device goes straight to the Repair Partner and the original seller does not see the claim. On Route A (the customer has RC+ and the original seller finds misuse within 1 year), the original seller still gives the quotation and repairs the device. [p1] [p3]
- ★ All refunds, credits and charges appear in the payment tool. That is where to check them on a warranty claim. [p1] [p3]

### Screens and fields

- ★ At Under QC the seller has two options: Mark VALID or Mark INVALID. [p1]
- ★ Claim info displayed in the portal: Tracking (original shipment), Pickup Tracking, Ship-Back and Lab. [p1]
