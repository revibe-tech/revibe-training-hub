## Summary

This video shows sellers how to use the Orders App on the Revibe admin site (admin.revibe.me): reading the Orders list and its stage groups, opening an order, editing it, and confirming availability. It then covers the airway bill and tracking ID steps by country, the 48-hour shipping rule and how courier data is used to flag late orders, and the Request Revibe box. It ends with the Invoices page, the Payment Tool, and the score KPIs. It is written for new sellers. The video is marked TBU (to be updated), so the workflow, figures and examples need checking before use.

## What a KAM needs to know

### Rules and policies

- ★ Orders are grouped by stage on the Order List: 02. Order created, 03. At quality check, 04. Shipped, 05. Delivered. [00:29; t00030] [t00046] [t00059]
- ★ When you receive an order, confirming its availability is the first step for every order. [04:29-04:33] [10:27-10:32]
- ★ For UAE and South Africa orders, confirming availability is also the final step; the seller only needs to confirm availability. [04:35-04:40]
- ★ Setting Availability Status to Not Available charges 5% of your cost. [05:00 t00300] [05:02]
- If an order has to be cancelled, the trainer says a 5% charge applies to these orders (trainer's statement, unconfirmed; the cost base and the trigger are not shown). [10:33-10:36]
- ★ The seller is the only one responsible for their prices, which is why the Not Available charge exists. [05:13]
- ★ For UAE and South Africa orders, an airway bill is generated automatically once availability is confirmed. [06:02-06:10] [07:24]
- ★ DHL must scan the shipment at the seller's location at the moment of pickup. [06:52]
- ★ Do not let DHL take the shipment to the warehouse to scan it a few hours later. [06:57-07:00]
- ★ The seller has 48 hours to ship the order. [07:04]
- ★ For Saudi Arabia orders, the seller must enter the tracking ID once availability is confirmed and the order is at quality check. [07:35-07:45] [07:30]
- ★ For Saudi Arabia orders, the tracking label link is optional. [07:53]
- ★ Revibe teams can only support a seller if the request is seen in the Request Revibe box in the section mentioned. [03:56-04:04]
- ★ Payments are made every time an order is shipped (trainer's statement). [11:24] (unconfirmed)
- Late orders hurt the seller's score. [07:14] (The trainer's example is one seller's very late order; the threshold is the 48-hour rule above.)
- A better seller score helps the seller get better prices from Revibe (trainer's statement). [11:55-12:01] (unconfirmed)
- ★ Orders move from 03. At quality check to 04. Shipped automatically, based on the courier's scan data. [08:04-08:27] [08:24 t00504]
