## What a KAM needs to know

### Rules and policies

- ★ Customers can return a device within 15 days after delivery. Eligibility: 15 days from delivery. [00:19] [00:00 t00000]
- ★ A second valid claim on the same order is accepted even after the 15-day period. [00:25] [00:33] [00:20 t00020]
- ★ For AE and ZA orders (trainer: UAE and South Africa), the airway bill is created automatically and the device is collected from the customer and delivered to the seller. The seller has no action for this pickup. [01:09] [01:15] [01:20 t00080]
- ★ For SA orders (trainer: Saudi Arabia), the seller creates the airway bill manually so the device is collected and delivered to the seller. [01:20] [01:27] [01:20 t00080]
- ★ If the Revibe Lab decision is Valid, the customer receives a full refund and the device is shipped back to the seller. [02:52] [03:01] [03:00 t00180]
- ★ If the Revibe Lab decision is Invalid, the customer pays and the device is shipped to the customer; if the customer does not pay, Revibe keeps the device. [03:02] [03:09] [03:13] [03:00 t00180]
- ★ A Battery draining claim needs a screenshot showing the % of Battery Health of the phone. The customer form links to a guide, "How to provide valid proof?" [04:05 t00242]
- ★ Customer notice: include all necessary items and send the device back in its original box. Failing to comply may result in the claim being delayed or rejected. [04:23 t00261]
- The customer form's testing options: Submit stays greyed until one option is selected. [04:23 t00261]
- If Revibe marks the customer claim Valid while the seller's proof is not sufficient, the claim goes back to the seller at Under revision; the seller then chooses Valid claim, Invalid claim or Not replied. [08:21 t00497] [08:28 t00518]
