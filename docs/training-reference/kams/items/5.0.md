## Summary

This short training (3:23) is for sellers and covers the cancellation policy that Revibe had "just launched" when it was recorded, around May 2025 [00:00] [00:44 t00044]. It shows how to spot an order the customer has cancelled (a purple bar on its row, and an on-hold banner inside the order) and how to open its Cancellation claim [00:36 t00036] [00:44 t00044] [00:56 t00056]. The seller then either accepts with Mark valid, which cancels the order and refunds the customer, or rejects with Mark Invalid [01:00] [01:04] [01:22]. No answer within 24 hours counts as accepted; UAE and South Africa cancellations must be accepted at the Created stage and may be rejected at the quality check stage; Saudi Arabia cancellations can always be rejected; and any rejection means shipping within the next 24 hours [01:09] [01:31] [01:38] [01:53] [02:01] [02:07]. The trainer says rejecting needs a tracking ID, but his line about which countries this applies to is ambiguous [02:15] [02:22]. The demo was recorded on an admin dashboard at stage-admin.revibe.me, so a seller's own screens may look different [00:00 t00000].

### Rules and policies

- ★ While a cancelled order is on hold, the seller cannot edit it or do anything else with it. The seller must first respond to the cancellation claim that was created. [00:44] [00:51]
- ★ A seller has two ways to respond to a cancellation claim: accept it with 'Mark valid', or reject it with 'Mark Invalid'. [01:00] [01:04] [01:16 t00076]
- ★ Accepting a cancellation (Mark valid) cancels the order and refunds the customer. [01:22] [01:31]
- ★ For UAE and South Africa orders at the Created stage, the seller must always accept the cancellation (mark it valid). [01:31] [01:38]
- ★ For UAE and South Africa orders at the quality check stage, the seller may reject the cancellation (mark it invalid), but must then ship the order within the next 24 hours. [01:45] [01:53] [02:01]
- ★ For Saudi Arabia orders, the seller can always reject the cancellation, but must then ship the order within the next 24 hours. [02:01] [02:07] [02:15]
