## Summary

This video shows sellers how to answer a cancellation claim in the Revibe orders app. It covers how a cancelled order looks on the Orders list and in the order drawer, and the two answers a seller can give: mark the claim valid or invalid. It also covers the tracking ID requirement and the 24-hour rules for some countries. It is for sellers and the KAMs who support them. Several rules are garbled in the audio or conflict with the screen, so check the Problems found section before using any rule as fact.

## What a KAM needs to know

### Rules and policies

- Two situations lead a customer to cancel an order. The customer changed his mind and no longer wants the device. Or the shipment is delayed on the seller's side and the customer does not want to wait anymore. [00:15] [00:20] [00:24] ★
- While an order is on hold because of a cancellation claim, the trainer says the seller cannot edit it and cannot do anything with it. (unconfirmed: the screen shows greyed-out fields and an Edit button, so this rests on narration.) [00:48] [00:44 t00044]
- Marking a cancellation as valid refunds the customer and cancels the order. [01:25] [01:28] ★
- For UAE and South Africa orders at the quality check stage, the seller may mark the cancellation as invalid (reject it). [01:48] [01:56]
- When a cancellation is rejected (marked invalid) for a UAE or South Africa order at the quality check stage, the seller must ship the order within the next 24 hours. [01:57] [02:03]
- For Saudi Arabia orders, the seller always has the option to reject the cancellation, but must then ship the order within the next 24 hours. [02:04] [02:12]
- For UAE and South Africa orders, when the order is at the stage the audio reads as "{created?}", the seller must always mark the cancellation as valid. (unconfirmed: the stage name is garbled and no frame shows the stage list.) [01:32] [01:39]

### Procedures

- The seller must respond to a cancellation claim that has just been created. Answer it from the Claims table in the order drawer: click the Claim ID link to open the Claim Details tab. [00:52] [00:56 t00056] ★
- A cancellation claim has two answers. Accept marks the cancellation as valid (button labelled Mark valid). Reject marks the cancellation as invalid (button labelled Mark Invalid). [01:00] [01:05] [01:16 t00076] ★
- Marking a claim as invalid requires a tracking ID (unconfirmed: which countries require it is unclear). If none is on the order, the system shows the error and opens the Add tracking ID modal. [02:15] [02:19 t00139]
- If the order already has a tracking ID, the seller can press Mark Invalid directly without entering a tracking ID again. (unconfirmed: the words "tracking ID link" at 02:38 are unclear.) [02:31] [02:38]
- After a claim is handled, the order appears again in the order dashboard as a normal order, and the seller can edit it and treat it as a normal order. (unconfirmed: the screens do not show this result.) [02:40] [02:48] ★
