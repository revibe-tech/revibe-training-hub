## What a KAM needs to know

### Rules and policies

- ★ Fulfillment is restricted to sellers who cannot win orders with their buy box, and to new sellers who have just been onboarded [04:14] [04:22]. The trainer also says the restriction came after an earlier, wider policy [04:07] (medium).
- ★ Fulfillment is not available for Saudi Arabia. The trainer says it is available for the UAE, South Africa and other countries where Revibe starts shipping [02:04]. The Score logic slide shows a red X for Saudi Arabia and green checks for UAE and South Africa [02:48 t00168].
- ★ Score band "> 0%": direct shipping is allowed and fulfillment is allowed. There is no upgrade badge in this band [02:48 t00168].
- ★ Score band "0% to -5%": direct shipping is allowed. Fulfillment is allowed, with the score upgraded at 0% [02:48 t00168] [02:40].
- ★ Score band "< -5%": direct shipping is not allowed (red X). Fulfillment is allowed, with the score upgraded at 0% [02:48 t00168] [01:31].
- ★ A negative score is upgraded to 0% when a seller sells through fulfillment, which gives better prices [02:40] [02:48 t00168].
- ★ The trainer says a score is either null or positive, because a negative score is never taken into account for fulfillment [07:33] (unconfirmed: the word "score" is low-confidence and no score column is on screen).
- ★ The trainer says fulfillment has priority on direct shipping, which gives it priority on the buy box [02:15] [02:24] (medium).
- ★ The trainer says that when a seller is blocked, it is most of the time because quality is not good; Revibe then blocks direct shipping orders and enables only fulfillment [01:23] [01:31] [01:36] (medium).
- ★ The trainer says fulfillment gives fast delivery to customers in the UAE and South Africa [01:57].
- ★ The trainer says creating a shipment is the seller's role, and the KAM can assist the seller [08:21] [08:27].
- ★ The trainer says the requested products inside a shipment must be shipped by the seller to ReviveLab [11:16] (the name ReviveLab does not appear on screen).
- ★ Ignore the warranty plan rows. A seller cannot sell a warranty [05:15] [05:22].
- ★ The trainer says the KAM is not supposed to interact in this dashboard or app. The app is there to support the KAM and show what the seller can see [14:14] [14:21] [14:23] (medium).
- ★ The trainer says the only All inventory category "you care about" is Fulfillment [13:18].
- ★ The trainer says payment terms for fulfillment are the same as for direct shipping orders [17:36] (medium).
- ★ Bulk QC rejection: when QC finds poor quality on too many pieces, the whole shipment is rejected, to avoid wasting time on too many QCs [15:49] [15:56].
