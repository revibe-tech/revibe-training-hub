## Summary

This video shows a newly accepted seller what happens in Quality screening, the second onboarding stage. The seller finds the Fulfillment App link in the Seller Onboarding App, looks through the SKUs available list, builds a first shipment with two request lines, and then reads what goes into the box and how it is picked up. The last part covers the Quality guidelines and the four onboarding stages. It is written for sellers, and this page is for a team lead and new KAMs who need to explain the same steps and rules. The screen values are a snapshot from 8 August 2025 and should not be treated as current.

## What a KAM needs to know

### Rules and policies

- ★ Quality screening: once the profile is validated, the seller gets access to the Fulfillment App. [00:13; t00011]
- ★ Quality outcome: if quality is good on average, Revibe starts selling the devices, includes the seller in the payment cycle, and onboards them. If quality does not match expectations, Revibe returns the devices. [00:26; 00:33; 00:36; t00011]
- Warehouse inspection branch on the onboarding slide: a check leads to Sales + Onboarding, and an X leads to Return + No-onboarding. [t00011; t00640]
- ★ Only one shipment can be open at a time. [03:17; 07:46]
- ★ Put in the box all the devices that were required, not more and not less. [08:29; 08:35]
- ★ Do not put other models in the box. [08:37]
- ★ A model can be put in the shipment only if it has been requested on the app. [08:39]
- ★ A model that was not requested on the app is shipped back to the seller immediately. [08:42]
- ★ Include one USB-C cable with each device in the shipment. The trainer says the accepted cable is the one shown on the slide, and once says "or an original cable" as an alternative (09:03, spoken once). [06:56; 08:56; 09:03; t00425]
- ★ For laptops, include the original cables in the box. [07:06] (medium confidence: the trainer says "the original cables")
- The seller does not need to pay shipping costs. The video does not say which shipments this covers (unconfirmed scope). [02:38]
- The Packaging request tab is not needed for now. The trainer says it will be covered in a later training video. [06:16; 06:19]
- ★ Price rule: the price must beat the Buybox price by at least 1 AED. Setting it equal to 598 gives the error "Come on! Let's beat Buybox! Win by at least 1 AED." [03:42; 03:47; t00227]
- Quality guidelines: the requirement for each condition is in the Quality guidelines. [09:46; 09:53; t00592]
- ★ Refer to the Quality guidelines to understand the accepted quality, so that the shipment is not rejected. [10:28; 10:34]
- Quality standards: the warehouse only accepts quality that meets them. Otherwise the shipment does not get accepted. [10:34] (the speech says "your shipment does not get rejected")
- ★ Hardware: 100% fully functional, across all grades. [t00596]
- ★ All features of the device must work perfectly. [10:03]

- ★ Screen: Excellent and Very good allow no sign of use. Good allows slight scratches that may be visible but do not interfere when the appliance is switched on. [t00596] (medium confidence)
