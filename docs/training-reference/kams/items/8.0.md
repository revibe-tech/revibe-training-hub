## Summary

This video explains the warranty claim process from the seller side, as seen in the Revibe Admin app. Part 1 covers who repairs a device, how to find and read a claim, the pickup step, and the seller's Valid or Invalid decision with repair and ship-back. Part 2 covers the Invalid path, Revibe's review, the lab step (UAE only), the lab outcomes, Revibe Care+ (RC+) quotations, and cancelled or reopened claims. It is for sellers and new Key Account Managers (KAMs). Example claims on screen include NUVE086, SJRS808, MJSX592, VKDD197 and UXEQ435.

## What a KAM needs to know

### Rules and policies

- ★ Within 1 year with no misuse declared, the original seller covers the warranty and repairs the device. [00:14] [00:14 t00013]
- ★ After 1 year, or if the customer declares misuse, the Revibe Repair Partner takes the device and the original seller does not see the claim. [00:18] [00:24] [00:14 t00013]
- ★ Warranty claims are handled in the Admin app, in the same place as return claims. [00:27]
- ★ Steps 1 and 2 of the warranty process (customer raises the claim, Revibe agent check) need no action from the seller. [01:24] [01:24 t00084]
- ★ Within 1 year, the seller inspects the device and marks the claim Valid (repair it) or Invalid (reason and proof). Revibe reviews every Invalid claim. [02:41 t00161] [03:37 t00217]
- ★ To mark a claim invalid, the seller must give a reason and proof. [03:36]
- ★ A Revibe agent reviews every invalid claim. [04:04] [04:04 t00244]
- ★ If the Revibe agent agrees with the seller's invalid claim, the device goes to the lab. [04:07] [04:04 t00244]
- ★ If the Revibe agent challenges the invalid claim, the agent marks it Valid with a rationale. Then either the seller must cover it (the claim goes to Under revision), or, for a misuse claim where the customer has RC+, the seller gives a quotation (see page 3 of the slide). [04:09] [04:04 t00244]
- ★ Under revision: the seller must mark the claim Valid or Invalid again with more proof; the agent reviews again, and an Invalid answer goes to the lab. [04:24] [04:04 t00244]
- ★ The lab check is in the UAE only. [04:39] [04:40 t00280]
- ★ Outside AE there is no lab, and the agent's confirmation of the seller's Invalid is final. (Scope of "final" is unclear; see Problems found.) [04:40 t00280] [04:46]
- ★ Lab VALID: the customer is refunded and the device goes back to the seller. Route: Pending lab to seller collection → In transit lab to seller → Delivered to seller. [04:49] [04:49 t00289]
- ★ Lab INVALID: "Invalid claim confirmed", and the claim is closed. Revibe ships the device back in AE.
