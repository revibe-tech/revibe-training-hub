## Summary

This reference covers the Revibe Warranty - Wrong Device workflow, which handles a customer who received a different device than the one ordered. It is written for sellers and KAMs. The workflow runs in four steps, from the customer raising the claim through the seller's decision, and it has a dispute-resolution branch for the seller's VALID and INVALID decisions [p1]. The source is a single PDF page with no narration [p1]. A wrong-device claim has no warranty-period check, so it goes straight to the original seller or repair partner [p1]. The seller's two decisions are to mark the claim VALID or INVALID [p1].

## What a KAM needs to know

### Rules and policies

- ★ The wrong-device warranty claim has no warranty-period check; the claim goes straight to the original seller (or repair partner) [p1].
- ★ At the Revibe agent check, missing documents are requested, and if they are not provided the claim is Cancelled. Once validated, the request moves on with status Pending Collection. No seller action is required at this step [p1].
- ★ For AE and Others pickup, the AWB is auto-created (AE = Quiqup, Others = DHL) and the claim moves to Under Collection immediately [p1].
- ★ For SA pickup, the seller must create the AWB manually [p1].
- ★ For repair partner pickup, the AWB is always created automatically. The same line reads "SA = Naqel"; the screen does not explain what that label means for repair partners (unconfirmed) [p1].
- ★ After the seller submits proof, Revibe checks it. Good proof sends the device to the Lab. Rejected proof re-marks the claim Valid, and the customer gets a replacement or refund [p1].
- ★ Repair partner claims have no Lab step; Revibe decides [p1].
- ★ Revibe Lab final decision: if Valid, a full refund is given and the device is shipped back to the seller. If Invalid, the customer pays and the device is shipped to the customer; if the customer does not pay, the device is disposed [p1].
- ★ If delivery fails on ship-back, the agent offers a new AWB. If the ship-back is refused, the device is sent to disposal [p1].
- ★ All refunds, credits and charges for this workflow appear in the payment tool [p1].

### Procedures

- The customer raises the wrong-device claim in the "My Account" section on Revibe. The claim status is Pending, reviewed by the Revibe team. No seller or KAM action is required at this step [p1].
- ★ Once the courier company delivers the claim to the seller, the seller can see it and its status becomes Under QC. The seller must then mark the claim VALID or INVALID [p1].
- ★ VALID path: the seller confirms stock of the right device. If the device is available, the correct device is shipped to the customer. If it is not available, an automated refund is issued and charged to the seller at actual cost, and the case is closed [p1].
- ★ INVALID path: the seller must submit proof, consisting of a written reason plus supporting proof, to challenge the customer claim [p1].

### Definitions

- Wrong device claim: the customer received a different device than ordered, under warranty. No warranty-period check applies [p1].
- AWB: the shipping document number for the pickup. The screen does not expand the abbreviation [p1].
- Dispute resolution: the section headed DISPUTE RESOLUTION, placed after the seller's decision, with a VALID box and an INVALID box [p1].
- Lab: Revibe Lab, which verifies the device and makes the final decision. It is not used for repair partner claims [p1].
- Repair partner: the repair partner channel, where pickup is always automatic, there is no Lab step, and Revibe decides [p1].
- Payment tool: where all refunds, credits and charges appear [p1].
