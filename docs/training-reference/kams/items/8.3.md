## Summary

This one-page workflow diagram, "Revibe Warranty – Wrong Device", is for sellers and KAMs and covers the whole wrong-device warranty process from start to finish [p1]. It applies when a customer received a different device from the one they ordered, under warranty. There is no warranty-period check, and the claim goes straight to the original seller (or repair partner) [p1]. The customer raises the claim in "My Account", a Revibe agent checks the proof, and pickup follows: the AWB is created automatically for AE (Quiqup) and Others (DHL), manually by the seller for SA, and always automatically for a repair partner (SA = Naqel) [p1]. Once the Courier Company delivers, the claim is Under QC and the seller marks it Valid (replacement if the right device is in stock, otherwise an automated refund charged to the seller) or Invalid (written reason and proof, then Revibe review: rejected proof → re-marked Valid; good proof → final Lab decision; for a repair partner, no Lab and Revibe decides) [p1]. The page gives no deadlines and leaves several points open; these are listed under Problems found [p1].

### Rules and policies

- ★ Wrong-device claims have no warranty-period check. [p1]
- ★ A wrong-device claim goes straight to the original seller (or repair partner). [p1]
- ★ Step 1 (Customer raise claim) and Step 2 (Revibe Agent Check) are both labelled NO ACTION REQUIRED. [p1]
- (unconfirmed) Implied: the action labels on the step cards are addressed to the seller. Step 3's ACTION REQUIRED IF SA matches "SA: Seller to create the AWB manually", and Step 4's ACTION REQUIRED card is titled "Seller action". So Steps 1 and 2 (NO ACTION REQUIRED) need no action from the seller. The page does not say this outright (see Unclear passages). [p1]
- ★ If documents are missing from the customer's proof, the Revibe agent requests them. If they are not provided, the claim is Cancelled. [p1]
- ★ Step 3 (Pickup) is labelled ACTION REQUIRED IF SA. For SA, the seller creates the AWB manually. [p1]
- ★ For AE & Others, the pickup AWB is created automatically. [p1]
- ★ For AE, the automatically created pickup AWB is with Quiqup. [p1]
- ★ For Others, the automatically created pickup AWB is with DHL. [p1]
- ★ When the claim goes to a repair partner, the pickup AWB is always created automatically, including in SA. [p1]
- ★ For a repair partner in SA, the automatically created pickup AWB is with Naqel. [p1]
- ★ Step 4 (Seller action) is labelled ACTION REQUIRED: the seller must act at this stage. [p1]
- ★ On a VALID wrong-device claim, if the right device is available, the correct device is shipped to the customer. [p1]
- ★ On a VALID wrong-device claim, if the right device is not available, an automated refund is issued and charged to the seller (actual cost). [p1]
- ★ On a VALID claim with the right device not available, the case is closed after the automated refund charged to the seller. [p1]
- ★ To mark a wrong-device claim INVALID, the seller must submit a written reason plus supporting proof to challenge the customer claim. [p1]
- ★ When Revibe checks the seller's proof on an INVALID claim, good proof means the device is sent to the Lab. [p1]
- ★ If Revibe rejects the seller's proof, the claim is re-marked Valid and goes to replacement or refund. [p1]
- ★ When the claim is with a repair partner and is marked INVALID, there is no Lab step and Revibe decides. [p1]
- ★ When Revibe accepts the seller's proof on an INVALID claim (good proof → device sent to Lab), the Revibe Lab makes the final decision. [p1]
- ★ If the Revibe Lab decides Valid, there is a full refund and the device is shipped back to the seller. [p1]
- ★ If the Revibe Lab decides Invalid and the customer pays, the device is shipped to the customer. [p1]
- ★ If the Revibe Lab decides Invalid and the customer doesn't pay, the device is disposed of. [p1]
- ★ After a failed ship-back delivery the agent offers a new AWB; if refused, the device is sent to disposal. [p1]

### Procedures

- ★ The customer raises a wrong-device claim through the "My Account" section on Revibe. [p1]
- In Step 2, a Revibe agent reviews the customer's proof for the wrong-device claim. [p1]
- ★ For SA, the seller must create the pickup AWB manually. [p1]
- ★ At Under QC, the seller has two options: Mark VALID or Mark INVALID. [p1]
- ★ If the seller marks the claim VALID, the outcome is Replacement or Refund, and the seller confirms whether the right device is in stock. [p1]
- ★ If the seller marks the claim INVALID, the path is Proof Required → Revibe Review → Lab Verification. [p1]
- ★ If delivery fails on ship-back, the agent offers a new AWB. [p1]

### Unclear passages

- [p1] The page does not spell out AE and SA (they look like market or country codes), and it does not say which markets count as "Others". Expand them only after confirming with the trainer.
- [p1] SA pickup: the page says the seller creates the AWB manually but not where or how, by when, or whether the status then moves to Under Collection as it does for AE & Others.
- [p1] Repair partner pickup: only the SA courier is given (Naqel). The courier for repair partners in AE or other markets is not stated.
- [p1] "charged to the seller (actual cost)": the page does not define what "actual cost" covers (device price, order value, shipping, etc.).
- [p1] Lab decision Invalid, "customer pays = ship to customer": the page does not say what the customer pays for (e.g. shipping or a fee) or how much.
- [p1] Lab decision Valid, "full refund + ship back to seller": the page does not say who bears the cost of that full refund (e.g. whether it is charged to the seller).
- [p1] "Delivery failed on ship-back": the page does not say whether this covers ship-back to the seller, to the customer, or both, or who does the refusing (the new AWB offer or the delivery).
- [p1] No deadline is given for the seller to mark Valid/Invalid at Under QC or to submit proof, and no deadline is given for the customer to provide missing docs.
- [p1] "Repair partner: no Lab, Revibe decides": the page does not say what follows each Revibe decision for a repair partner (refund, ship-back, disposal).
- [p1] The action labels (NO ACTION REQUIRED / ACTION REQUIRED IF SA / ACTION REQUIRED) do not name who they apply to. They read as seller actions (Step 3: "SA: Seller to create the AWB manually"; Step 4: "Seller action"), but the page does not say so outright.
- [p1] VALID path, "Available → correct device shipped to the customer": the page does not say who ships the correct device, who pays for that shipment, or what happens to the wrong device that was collected.
- [p1] SA pickup vs seller visibility: Step 3 says that for SA the seller creates the pickup AWB manually, which happens before the courier delivers, yet Step 4 says "Once the Courier Company delivers the seller can see the claim". The page does not say how an SA seller sees the claim (or is told about it) at Pending Collection, or where they create the AWB. Confirm with the trainer before quizzing fact 18 for SA.
- [p1] Scope: the banner says both "under warranty" and "no warranty-period check" but does not explain how the two fit together, or whether any other time limit applies to a wrong-device claim. Do not teach that wrong-device claims have no time limit at all; confirm with the trainer.
