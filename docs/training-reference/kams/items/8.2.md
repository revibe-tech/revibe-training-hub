## Summary

This video teaches the wrong device warranty claim in the Revibe seller portal. The customer's claim is raised on Revibe, the seller checks the device received, and then marks the claim Valid or Invalid [00:08 t00008] [00:43 t00043]. A wrong device claim applies when the customer received a different device than the one they ordered, and there is no warranty-period check [00:15] [00:18 t00017]. It is for sellers and Key Account Managers (KAMs), as set in the item metadata (audience: Seller & KAM).

## What a KAM needs to know

### Rules and policies

- ★ A wrong-device claim follows the same four steps as any warranty claim. Only the outcome is different. [00:08 t00008]
- ★ Scope: the customer received a different device than the one ordered, under warranty. There is no warranty-period check. The claim goes straight to the original seller (or repair partner). [00:15] [00:18 t00017] [00:20 t00017]
- ★ Steps 1 (customer raises claim) and 2 (Revibe Agent Check) need no action from the seller. [00:24 t00025]
- ★ Step 2 can end the claim. The Revibe agent reviews the customer's proof: missing documents are requested, and if they are not provided the claim is Cancelled. Once validated, the request moves on. [00:24 t00025]
- ★ Pickup for AE and Others: the AWB is created automatically and the claim moves to Under Collection immediately. AE is Quiqup and Others is DHL. [00:33 t00033] [00:38 t00033]
- ★ Pickup for Saudi Arabia (SA): the seller creates the AWB manually. [00:40] [00:40 t00033]
- Repair partner pickup is always automatic (SA = Naqel). [00:33 t00033]
- ★ Seller action: once the courier delivers the device, the seller can see the claim, and its status becomes Under QC. The seller then chooses Mark VALID or Mark INVALID. [00:43 t00043] [00:08 t00008]
- ★ Decision: if the customer is right (the device received really is the wrong one), mark the claim Valid. If it is not a wrong device, mark it Invalid. [00:57] [01:48]
- ★ Replacement available (Yes): the correct device is shipped to the customer. The seller confirms stock of the right device before choosing Yes. [01:00 t00058] [01:14]
- ★ Replacement not available (No): Revibe refunds the customer automatically and charges the refund to the seller at actual cost. The case is closed and the seller keeps the device. There is no extra shipping. [01:38 t00099] [01:44 t00099]
- ★ Invalid claim proof: the required content is a written reason and supporting proof to challenge the customer claim. [01:48 t00110] [02:02 t00123]
- ★ The invalid path runs: seller submits proof, then Revibe Review, then Lab Verification. [01:48 t00110] [00:08 t00008]
- ★ Lab final decision, Valid: the customer gets a full refund and the device is shipped back to the seller. [02:18] [02:19 t00139]
- ★ If a ship-back delivery fails, the agent offers a new AWB. If the customer refuses, the device is sent to disposal. [02:28 t00150] (The slide says "new AWB". The narration says "a new attempt"; see Unclear passages.)
- ★ All refunds, credits and charges appear in the payment tool. [02:33] [02:28 t00150]
- ★ Questions about the wrong-device process go to your key account manager. [02:39] [02:37 t00157]
