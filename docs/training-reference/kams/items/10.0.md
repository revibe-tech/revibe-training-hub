## Summary

This video walks through Revibe's quality guidelines for phones, tablets, watches and laptops. The trainer says the guidelines are written so sellers can follow them, avoid as many claims as possible and spend less time handling claims [00:07-00:13 t00008]. It covers the three grades (Excellent, Very good, Good), the rules that apply to each grade, the screen acceptance rules, the banned-elements list and the claims limit with its penalty. It is for sellers and KAMs. Most of the rules are shown on the deck slides; the trainer mainly pages through them.

## What a KAM needs to know

### Rules and policies

- ★ Every device must be 100% functional in every grade, like a new one. The Hardware row reads "100% fully functional" across all grades. [00:20 t00021]
- ★ The difference from a new device must never be in the features. It may only be in small details such as battery health or physical condition. [00:29, 00:35 t00028]
- ★ Banned elements are not accepted in any grade and on any device, and should never lead to a claim. [03:16 t00197; 03:57 t00237]
- ★ If the supplier does a banned element, "the supplier clearly didn't follow the quality guidelines." [03:22 t00199]
- ★ Screen: Excellent and Very good must show no sign of use. [00:37 t00038]
- ★ Screen, Good: slight scratches are allowed, as long as you can't see them when the screen is on. (unconfirmed: the on-screen wording says they may be visible but do not interfere when the appliance is switched on.) [00:42 t00028]
- ★ Body, Excellent: no sign of use; the device should look exactly like a new device. [00:48, 00:51 t00049]
- Body, Very good and Good: may have a few light scratches that are not visible at first glance unless you look closely (marked Small). (unconfirmed: the narration adds "invisible from 20 to 30 centimeters away"; that distance is not on screen.) [00:54 t00049]
- ★ Plug or socket type must match the country's specs in every grade. The rule also applies to laptops. [01:24 t00086; 01:27]
- ★ Display: Excellent requires an original or DD display. Very good and Good accept a high-quality copy. [01:30, 01:38 t00092]
- ★ Glass, cover, buttons and others may be high-quality copies in every grade, but the device must never show a message about non-original parts. [01:43, 01:49 t00104]
- ★ On Samsung devices, the S Pen must be original in every grade. [01:51 t00112]
- ★ Functions are the same in every grade, because everything must be 100% functional. The only exception is the camera dots. [02:00 t00119; 02:10 t00130]
- ★ Data must have been reset (data deletion). [01:59 t00119]
- ★ Chargers and cables: an original copy is required. It must charge effectively and be compatible. [01:59 t00119]
- ★ Unlocking: the device must be unlocked from any previous protection systems. [01:59 t00119]
- ★ IMEI: the IMEI is checked to see whether it has been blacklisted, and the device configuration (like color and capacity) must match manufacturer settings. [01:59 t00119]
- ★ Temperature: the device must not overheat when used in normal conditions. [01:59 t00119]
- Phone carrier: "Must be unlocked to a phone carrier" (unconfirmed: the wording is ambiguous; see Problems found). [02:00 t00119]
- Other functions in the deep dive: SIM and memory cards must read correctly; external sensors, biometric sensors, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, GPS, flash and indicator lights, mics and speakers, and ports (headphone jacks, battery, USB, ethernet) must work; the oxidation indicator is inspected; buttons must feel like the originals and open the right functions; on-screen visuals must have no stains, dust or burn-in discolouration. [01:59 t00119]
- ★ Screens accepted: Original and DD screens are accepted. [02:18 t00136]
- ★ Replacement screens: a GW Good quality screen is accepted only on the Good grade. It is not accepted on Excellent or Very good. [02:20 t00136]
- ★ Replacement screens: a GW & others Bad quality screen is not accepted in any grade. [02:16 t00136]
- ★ Thick screens are not accepted, and the screen must sit flat on the device. [02:56, 02:58 t00179]
- ★ Screens with a pinkish or yellowish tint are refused. [03:01 t00184]
- Gaps around the display and an Apple logo that stays frozen on the screen are listed as other issues to look out for. [03:09 t00187]
- ★ Banned list, Grading: a device sold as "Excellent" with visible scratches is a banned element. [03:57, 03:58 t00237]
- ★ Banned list, Grading: a "New" phone must be non-active and sold in a box that has never been opened. [04:11 t00251]
- ★ Banned list, Wrong item: a wrong device shipped (wrong storage, colour or model) is a banned element. [04:18 t00257]
- ★ Banned list, Wrong item: a region variant mismatch, such as the Japan shutter sound or a device that cannot be used in the destination countries, is a banned element. [04:20 t00257]
- ★ Banned list, Wrong item: missing in-box accessories are a banned element, for example a charger for laptops or a cable for all devices. [04:26 t00257]
- ★ Banned list, Parts: a non-original or fake S Pen on a Samsung device is a banned element. [04:32 t00271]
- ★ Banned list, Parts: a device showing a spare-part replacement message (showing non-original parts) or a battery replacement alert is a banned element. [04:38 t00271]
- ★ Banned list, Locks & IMEI: an iCloud, Google account or MDM profile still active on the device is a banned element. [04:40 t00280]
- ★ Banned list, Locks & IMEI: a blacklisted IMEI, or a device reported lost or stolen, is a banned element. [04:40, 04:42 t00280]
- ★ Banned list, Locks & IMEI: a device not factory reset, still holding previous user data, is a banned element. [04:40, 04:46 t00280]
- ★ Banned list, Locks & IMEI: a network or carrier-locked (SIM lock) device sold as unlocked is a banned element. [04:40, 04:46 t00280]
- ★ Laptops: hardware must be 100% fully functional. [05:01 t00301]
- ★ Laptop screen: almost no sign of use, with only 1 or 2 very small scratches accepted. [05:01 t00301]
- ★ Laptop battery should work normally, with no error or replacement message. [05:01 t00301]
- Laptops: SIM cards and memory cards must be read correctly, and trays or slots must insert and remove the card properly. [05:01 t00301]
- Laptops: the most recent operating system must be installed and activated. [05:01 t00301]
- Laptops: an original charger is required. It must be clean, charge effectively and be compatible with the country socket. [05:01 t00301]
- Laptops: the device must be unlocked from any previous protection systems set by the previous owner, or third-party accounts or software. [05:01 t00301]
- Laptops: all cameras must be functional, capture image and quality, and work for video conferencing. [05:01 t00301]
- Laptops: ports (headphone jack, battery, USB, ethernet) must be functional. [05:01 t00301]
- Laptops: mechanical parts (screws, case, frames and others) must be genuine or high-quality compatible. Any compatible part found must be tested to function as well as a genuine part. [05:01 t00301]
- Laptops: on-screen visuals must have no stains, dust or burn-in discolouration. [05:01 t00301]
- Laptops: the Arabic keyboard can be laser or printed, but not stickers. [05:01 t00301]
- Laptops: Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, biometric sensors (Touch ID, Face ID, ID sensor, voice recognition), GPS, external and internal sensors (such as temperature), mics and speakers, fans and ventilation, and internal hardware (hard disk, SSD, graphics card) must be checked as listed on the slide. Trackpads, touch bars, keyboards and backlighting must be fully functional. [05:01 t00301]

### Procedures

- ★ Battery health is checked with 3utool, which the trainer describes as a free tool every seller can use. (unconfirmed: the reading and how it is matched are not explained.) [01:07 t00061]
- ★ To check camera dots, switch the camera to portrait mode and point it at white paper or a white wall. [01:12, 01:17 t00073; 02:07 t00127]
- ★ To spot a pinkish or yellowish tint, take a picture of the device from the side. [03:05 t00184]
- ★ AI analyses customer claim forms in detail and flags the issues it spots. You check the flagged claims, and if they are valid the seller gets a penalty for each one. [03:45, 03:49, 03:52 t00225]
- When the claims trigger is activated, the claims are checked one by one. (unconfirmed: the slide says the Revibe team checks them; the narration says "You, as a KM".) [03:27 t00207; 03:35]
- The IMEI serial number is checked for blacklisting, and the device configuration (like color and capacity) must match manufacturer settings. [05:01 t00301]
