### Definitions

- ★ WD (working day): the sum of all the hours during working days when the claim is pending only on the seller's side, meaning only the seller has to act to move the claim to the next stage. [01:46–01:55 t00133]
- ★ Seller clock: the accumulated sum of the working hours a claim spends in seller-owned stages. It runs while the claim sits with the seller, stops when it moves to a stage Revibe owns, and picks up again where it left off if it comes back. [09:25 t00565] [09:42 t00582]
- ★ Resolution request: what the seller creates when there is an issue on its side. It freezes the clock until the resolution is marked solved. [06:02 t00360] [02:10 t00133]
- AWB: the bill the seller creates in SA. The card says the AWB is the seller's to create and nothing moves until it is issued. The expansion is not shown or spoken (the narration says "airway bill"). [03:11 t00192] [05:03 t00301]
- Under collection: the period in which the customer has to hand the device to the delivery company. [03:21 t00201]
- Stopped (timer): hours are banked and the claim is frozen in that column until the timer restarts. Moving (timer): the timer is running and hours accrue. [10:00 t00600] [10:24 t00624]
- ★ Policy day: 24 working hours, counting only the seller's hours (Methodology text). A chart note and the drawer footnote say 24 weekday hours on the Dubai calendar day. (conflict: see Problems found) [09:47 t00587] [09:25 t00565] [10:00 t00600] [10:24 t00624]
