# Quiz plan

This page covers how many questions to write for each item, what to ask about, which areas suit multiple-answer questions, an order for new joiners, and the 20 strongest scenario seeds. It is built from the quiz blueprints on the topic pages ([5. Cancellations](topics/5-cancellations.md), [8. Warranties](topics/8-warranties.md), [13. Commitments](topics/13-commitments.md)) and the Quiz material on the item pages.

Pierre asked for at most 20 questions per video, a mix of single-answer and multiple-answer questions, written by people rather than generated. This plan supports that; it doesn't replace it. It suggests numbers and areas and points to the checked facts. Writing the questions is up to the people writing the quiz.

Citations: `5.0 [01:31]` = said at 1:31 in item 5.0. `5.0 [00:44 t00044]` = on screen, in the frame taken at 0:44. `8.1 [p2]` = printed on page 2 of the PDF.

## Before you write

- Build each question from a ★ fact on the item page, and open its citation first.
- Leave out anything marked (unconfirmed), anything on the "Keep out" line under each table below, and anything the topic page lists under Contradictions.
- **Be fair to the 5.0 trainer.** He is a French speaker, and speech recognition misheard some of his accented English. For example, it heard "on your site" for "on your side" (5.0 [00:24]). If a question quotes him, use the "Should be" column of the Speech recognition fixes table on the [5.0 page](items/5.0.md), never the raw transcript. Test the rule, not his phrasing. Where the meaning of a line is unclear (5.0 [02:22] [02:38]), don't test it. At [02:22] speech recognition heard every word clearly; the sentence itself contradicts itself. At [02:38], "link" may be misheard. Ask him what he meant before using either line.
- 13.0's narration is clear. Its two uncertain words are confirmed on screen (13.0 [00:05] [00:12]). 8.1 and 8.3 are PDFs with no narration, so speech recognition doesn't affect them.
- In warranty questions, name the claim type. Issues (8.1) and wrong device (8.3) go through the same four opening steps, but their pickup rules differ at Step 3 (topic 8, Contradiction 4, plus 8.3's always-automatic pickup for a repair partner), and their paths split after the seller decides.
- Test the process, not one-off dates. Write 13.0's dates as "in the round shown", or leave them out.

## The 20 strongest scenario seeds

These come from the scenario seeds on the item pages and the "best 3" seeds on the topic pages. Each one asks what to do in a situation, rests on facts the checkers confirmed, and avoids the keep-out points. All 12 topic-page picks are included, marked *(topic pick)*. They follow the learning order above. The situations are written in plain words, not in the trainer's words.

### 5.0 Cancellations

1. **A seller sees a small purple bar on the left of an order row and can't edit the order.** → The customer cancelled the order, and it is on hold. Open the order, click the Claim ID link in the Claims section, and respond on the Claim Details tab with Mark valid or Mark Invalid (5.0 [00:36 t00036] [00:44] [00:51] [00:56 t00056] [01:00] [01:04]).
   - *Format:* single answer for what to do, or multiple answer for the signs. *Watch out:* the admin demo shows an Edit button on the on-hold order (5.0 [00:44 t00044]). Test "respond to the claim first", not what that screen shows.
2. *(topic pick)* **A UAE customer cancels while the order is still at the Created stage.** → The seller must Mark valid. The order is cancelled and the customer refunded (5.0 [01:31] [01:38] [01:22]).
   - *Format:* single answer.
3. *(topic pick)* **A South Africa order is at the quality check stage and the seller wants to keep the sale.** → The seller may Mark Invalid, but must then ship within the next 24 hours (5.0 [01:45] [01:53] [02:01]).
   - *Format:* single answer. *Watch out:* leave the tracking ID out of the answer, because the line on which countries need one is unclear (5.0 [02:22]).
4. **A Saudi Arabia customer cancels, and the seller can still ship.** → The seller can always reject a Saudi Arabia cancellation (Mark Invalid), but must then ship within the next 24 hours (5.0 [02:01] [02:07] [02:15]).
   - *Format:* single answer. *Watch out:* as in seed 3, leave the tracking ID out. The unclear line at 5.0 [02:22] names Saudi Arabia.
5. *(topic pick)* **A seller didn't answer a cancellation claim for more than 24 hours.** → It counts as valid: the order is cancelled and the customer refunded (5.0 [01:09] [01:16] [01:22]).
   - *Format:* multiple answer (cancelled; refunded).

### 8.1 Warranties - Issues

6. *(topic pick)* **An AE seller receives a device at Under QC within 1 year and finds a genuine fault.** → Mark VALID, repair it and click "Repair finished". Revibe makes the ship-back AWB, and 30 AED is deducted from the seller (8.1 [p1] [p2]).
   - *Format:* single or multiple answer. *Watch out:* set it in AE. Whether ZA sellers pay 30 AED or a local amount is unclear (8.1 [p2]).
7. **An SA customer didn't hand over the device at pickup and wants another try.** → Collection failed. The agent asks if the customer wants a new AWB. In SA the seller creates the new AWB manually, and the claim goes back to Under collection. If the customer says no, the claim is Cancelled (8.1 [p1]).
   - *Format:* single answer.
8. **An AE seller marks a claim INVALID with a reason and proof of invalidity, and the Revibe agent agrees.** → The status is Pending lab collection. A Quiqup AWB is created automatically, and the seller hands the device to Quiqup. If the Lab finds the claim Valid, the customer is refunded and the device goes back to the seller (8.1 [p2]).
   - *Format:* single answer. *Watch out:* keep it in AE (topic 8, Contradiction 1).
9. *(topic pick)* **Within 1 year, a seller finds misuse, but the customer has RC+.** → The seller marks Invalid (misuse). The agent marks "Misuse but customer has RC+". The original seller then gives a quotation and repairs the device, and Revibe covers the cost (8.1 [p2] [p3]).
   - *Format:* single answer. *Watch out:* don't ask whether the 1,500 AED cap applies on this route (8.1 [p3]).
10. **A seller asks why they can't see a warranty claim that falls after the 1-year window.** → After 1 year, or when the customer declares misuse, the device goes straight to the Revibe Repair Partner, and the original seller doesn't see the claim (8.1 [p1] [p3]).
    - *Format:* single answer, or multiple answer on what sends a claim to the partner. *Watch out:* don't ask which date the year counts from (8.1 [p1]).
11. *(topic pick)* **An RC+ repair quote comes in above 1,500 AED.** → The status is Pending customer payment, and the customer pays (cost − 1,500 AED). If they pay, the repair goes ahead. If they don't, there is no repair, the device is shipped back to the customer and RC+ is cancelled (8.1 [p3]).
    - *Format:* multiple answer.

### 8.3 Warranties - Wrong Device

12. **A seller asks whether they have to book the pickup for a wrong-device claim at Pending Collection.** → Only in SA, where the seller creates the AWB manually (Step 3 is ACTION REQUIRED IF SA). For AE (Quiqup) and Others (DHL), the AWB is created automatically and the claim moves to Under Collection immediately. For a repair partner it is always automatic (SA = Naqel) (8.3 [p1]).
    - *Format:* multiple answer. *Watch out:* don't ask how or when an SA seller sees the claim at this stage. Don't mix in 8.1's "AE & ZA" (topic 8, Contradiction 4).
13. *(topic pick)* **A wrong-device claim reaches Under QC, and the seller agrees it is the wrong device.** → Mark VALID and check stock of the right device. If it's available, the correct device is shipped to the customer. If not, an automated refund is charged to the seller (actual cost) and the case is closed (8.3 [p1]).
    - *Format:* single answer per branch. *Watch out:* don't ask what "actual cost" covers.
14. *(topic pick)* **A seller believes the customer's wrong-device claim isn't justified.** → Mark INVALID with a written reason and supporting proof. Revibe checks it. Good proof → the device is sent to the Lab. Rejected proof → re-marked Valid → replacement or refund. For a repair partner there is no Lab, and Revibe decides (8.3 [p1]).
    - *Format:* multiple answer. *Watch out:* set it in AE (topic 8, Contradiction 1), and say "wrong device". On an issue claim, a challenged Invalid can go to Under revision instead (8.1 [p2]).
15. *(topic pick)* **A disputed wrong device is with the Revibe Lab.** → The Lab makes the final decision. Valid → full refund, and the device is shipped back to the seller. Invalid → the device is shipped to the customer if they pay, and disposed of if they don't (8.3 [p1]).
    - *Format:* single answer per outcome. *Watch out:* set it in AE. Don't ask who bears the cost of the refund. Lab Invalid differs from issue claims, where Revibe ships the device back in AE (8.1 [p2]).
16. **A seller asks where to see a refund they were charged on a warranty claim.** → All refunds, credits and charges appear in the payment tool (8.3 [p1]; 8.1 [p1] [p3]).
    - *Format:* single answer. It holds for both claim types.

### 13.0 Commitments

17. *(topic pick)* **A seller saved their offer and thinks the commitment is now accepted.** → Not yet. After submissions close, Revibe reviews the offers and confirms the accepted commitments with the seller (13.0 [00:07 t00007]).
    - *Format:* single answer. *Watch out:* this rests on the on-screen text only. Don't ask when accepted commitments start to apply or how sellers are told (13.0 [00:00]).
18. *(topic pick)* **A seller only wants to commit to 2 of the 19 devices open.** → They fill in price and quantity on those two, or click "Use suggested", and leave the rest empty. Only filled devices count in the offer (13.0 [00:28] [00:33] [00:07 t00007] [00:32 t00032] [00:20 t00020]).
    - *Format:* multiple answer (true statements about filling in an offer).
19. *(topic pick)* **A seller split 200 units by colour. They see a red "120 / 200" and "1 line to fix", and the device is missing from their offer total.** → The colours must add up to the quantity. Once they reach "200 / 200", the device counts again (13.0 [00:38] [00:39 t00039] [00:43 t00043]).
