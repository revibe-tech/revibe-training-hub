# Glossary

Every term that the four item pages define or rely on: [5.0 Cancellations (Seller)](items/5.0.md), [8.1 Warranties - Issues (Seller & KAMs)](items/8.1.md), [8.3 Warranties - Wrong Device (Seller & KAMs)](items/8.3.md) and [13.0 Commitments](items/13.0.md). Each meaning is how the material uses the term, not a general definition. Where the pages mark something as unclear or unconfirmed, the entry says so. Where the pages define a term differently, the entry says "Differs", and the differences are also listed [below the table](#where-the-pages-differ).

Citations: `5.0 [01:00]` = item 5.0, said at 01:00. `[00:44 t00044]` = on screen in frame t00044. `8.1 [p2]` = PDF page 2. Items 8.1 and 8.3 are PDFs with no narration. Item 5.0 is narrated by a French speaker, and speech recognition misheard some of his words. Entries use the corrected wording, and "Speech:" notes what was heard. The full list of mishearings is [at the end](#speech-recognition-and-the-trainers-accent).

## Where the pages differ

- **Mark valid / Mark Invalid** mean different things for the three kinds of claim. In 5.0 they accept or reject a customer's cancellation, and rejecting commits the seller to ship within 24 hours. In 8.1 Valid means repair and ship back, and Invalid means giving proof that Revibe reviews. In 8.3 Valid means replacement or a refund charged to the seller, and Invalid means giving proof that Revibe reviews. 5.0 [01:00] [01:04]; 8.1 [p2]; 8.3 [p1]
- **Market labels:** 5.0 and 13.0 name countries. 8.1 uses AE / ZA / SA, and 8.3 uses AE / SA / Others. The automatic pickup AWB applies to "AE & ZA" in 8.1 but to "AE & Others" in 8.3. 8.1 [p1]; 8.3 [p1]
- **Repair partner:** in 8.1 it is the Revibe Repair Partner (RC+), used after 1 year or when the customer declares misuse. In 8.3 it is a repair partner that is not linked to RC+, whose pickup AWB is always automatic and whose disputed claims are decided by Revibe without a Lab. 8.1 [p1] [p3]; 8.3 [p1]
- **Lab:** 8.1 says the Lab exists only in AE ("No Lab yet" elsewhere). 8.3 states no market limit but has no Lab for a repair partner. 8.1 [p2]; 8.3 [p1]
- **Lab Invalid:** in 8.1, Revibe ships the device back in AE, and ZA & SA customers pay the shipping fees within 90 days. In 8.3, the device is shipped to the customer only if they pay, and is disposed of if they don't. 8.1 [p2]; 8.3 [p1]
- **Delivery failed on ship-back:** 8.1 says the seller pays for the new AWB on a Valid claim. 8.3 does not say who pays. 8.1 [p2]; 8.3 [p1]
- **Quiqup:** 8.1 names it for lab collection in AE. 8.3 names it for the AE pickup. 8.1 [p2]; 8.3 [p1]
- **Ship-back:** in 8.1 it means the return to the customer. 8.3 also uses it for "ship back to seller". 8.1 [p2]; 8.3 [p1]
- **Warranty period:** 8.1 routes claims by the 1-year window. 8.3 has "no warranty-period check". 8.1 [p1]; 8.3 [p1]
- **Claim, Cancelled, Tracking and Payment** each appear in more than one item with a different scope: a cancellation claim vs a warranty claim, a cancelled order vs a cancelled warranty claim, 5.0's Tracking ID vs 8.1's Tracking and Pickup Tracking, and the payment tool vs the 'Payment Tool' menu item (5.0) vs the 'Payment' tab (13.0). No page links them.
